## FAQ

### Q: Is a locker included with Aquaverse tickets?
Lockers are included free with online advance tickets. Gate tickets do not include a locker (THB 300 extra).

### Q: Are towels provided at Aquaverse?
Towels are not included with any ticket. Bring your own or rent one at the park for THB 107 plus a THB 300 refundable deposit.

### Q: Is swimwear available to buy at Aquaverse?
Yes. Retail shops inside the park sell swimwear, goggles, sunscreen, and swim accessories. Prices are higher than outside shops.

### Q: Can I get a refund on Aquaverse tickets?
Yes, free cancellation is available on most online bookings. Refunds are typically processed within 5-10 business days.

### Q: Is there a child ticket price at Aquaverse?
Children under 3 enter free. Children aged 3-12 and seniors 65+ may receive a discounted gate price of THB 1,290. Online tickets use a single price for all ages 3+, starting from THB 1,176.

### Q: What payment methods are accepted for online booking?
Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX), PayPal, and various local payment methods. At the gate, cash (Thai Baht) and credit cards are accepted.
