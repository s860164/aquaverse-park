    Save More

    5 Money-Saving Tips for Aquaverse

    Get the most value from your visit with these insider tips.

    💰

    1. Book Online to Save Up to 45%

    The biggest savings come from booking in advance online. Standard admission drops from THB 1,595 (gate) to just THB 870 online — and your locker is included free.

    📅

    2. Visit on Weekdays for Shorter Queues

    Weekdays (especially Monday to Thursday, excluding Wednesday when the park is closed) are significantly less crowded. You'll spend more time on rides and less time waiting.

    📱

    3. Use the Cashless RFID Wristband System

    Load credit onto your RFID wristband to buy food, drinks, and souvenirs without carrying cash or cards around the water park. Easy to track spending too.

    🏖️

    4. Skip the Cabana If on a Budget

    VIP cabanas are nice but not essential. There are plenty of shaded areas around the park, and the standard ticket already includes locker access when booked online.

    ⏰

    5. Arrive at 10 AM to Maximize Value

    The park opens at 10 AM and closes at 6 PM. Arriving at opening gives you a full 8 hours, shorter queues in the morning, and the best experience for your money.
