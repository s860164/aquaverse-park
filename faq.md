# Aquaverse Frequently Asked Questions

> Answers to 40+ common questions about visiting Columbia Pictures Aquaverse in Pattaya, Thailand.

## Key Facts

- **Location:** Na Jomtien, Sattahip, Chonburi, Thailand
- **Hours:** 10:00 AM -- 6:00 PM daily (closed Wednesdays)
- **Tickets:** THB 1,176 online / THB 1,595 gate
- **Phone:** +66 (0) 33 004 999

---

## Tickets & Pricing

### Q: How much do Aquaverse tickets cost?
Walk-in gate price is THB 1,595 (~$44 USD) per person. Online advance tickets start from THB 1,176 (~$24 USD), saving up to ~27%. Children under 3 enter free. Online tickets include locker rental (worth THB 300).

### Q: How much are children's tickets at Aquaverse?
Children under 3 enter free. Children aged 3-12 and seniors 65+ may receive a discounted gate price of THB 1,290 (vs THB 1,590 for adults). Online tickets typically use a single price for all ages 3+, starting from THB 1,176.

### Q: Is it cheaper to buy tickets online or at the gate?
Online tickets are significantly cheaper. Gate price is THB 1,595 while online prices start from THB 1,176 -- that's up to ~27% off. Online tickets also include a free locker rental (worth THB 300) and instant mobile voucher confirmation.

### Q: Is locker rental included with Aquaverse tickets?
Locker rental is included free with online advance tickets. If you buy tickets at the gate, locker rental costs an additional THB 300. Lockers are located near the entrance and can be accessed unlimited times throughout the day.

### Q: Are there discounts or promotions for Aquaverse?
Yes. Best discounts are available through online booking platforms (Klook, KKday, Tiquets) offering up to ~27% off gate price. Thai nationals may receive special rates with valid Thai ID. Holiday promotions run during Songkran, New Year, and other holidays.

### Q: Does Aquaverse offer group pricing?
Yes, group pricing is available for parties of 10 or more. Contact the park at +66 (0) 33 004 999 for group rate inquiries. School groups, corporate outings, and tour operators may receive additional discounts.

### Q: How much does a cabana cost at Aquaverse?
Cabana options: Deluxe THB 2,500/day, Super Deluxe THB 3,000/day, VIP THB 3,500/day. Includes shade, comfortable seating, towels, a personal attendant, and priority food service.

### Q: Can I get a refund on Aquaverse tickets?
Most online platforms (Klook, KKday) offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before the visit date. Gate-purchased tickets are generally non-refundable.

## Opening Hours & Schedule

### Q: What are the opening hours of Aquaverse?
Open daily from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Rides close at 5:45 PM. Last admission is at 3:30 PM. Closed every Wednesday for scheduled maintenance.

### Q: Is Aquaverse closed on Wednesdays?
Yes. Aquaverse is closed every Wednesday for scheduled maintenance. The park operates normally on all other days.

### Q: What time is last admission at Aquaverse?
Last admission is at 3:30 PM. All rides close at 5:45 PM and park gates close at 6:00 PM. Arrive at 10 AM opening to maximize your time.

### Q: Is Aquaverse open on Thai public holidays?
Yes, Aquaverse is generally open on Thai public holidays (except if the holiday falls on a Wednesday). Holiday periods tend to be busier with potentially extended hours.

### Q: When is the best time to visit Aquaverse?
- **Best day:** Weekdays (Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri) are least crowded
- **Best season:** November to April (dry season) for the best weather
- **Best arrival time:** 10 AM at gate opening for shortest queues
- **Avoid:** Weekends, Thai school holidays, and Songkran week

## Getting There & Parking

### Q: How do I get to Aquaverse from Bangkok?
- By car: 1.5-2 hours via Motorway Route 7 (free parking at park)
- By bus: From Ekkamai Bus Terminal (next to Ekkamai BTS), then taxi from Pattaya
- By shared van: From Victory Monument or Ekkamai, ~THB 240/person
- By Grab/taxi: THB 1,200-1,500 + tolls from Bangkok

### Q: How do I get to Aquaverse from Pattaya?
Aquaverse is about 30 minutes south of Pattaya city center in Na Jomtien. By Grab/taxi: THB 300-500 (20-30 min). By songthaew: Take the Na Jomtien route. Some hotels arrange transfers for an extra charge.

### Q: Is there a shuttle bus to Aquaverse?
No, Aquaverse does not operate an official shuttle bus. Best option from Pattaya is Grab (THB 300-500, 20-30 min). Some hotels and tour operators offer private transfers for an extra charge.

### Q: Is parking free at Aquaverse?
Yes, parking is completely free for all guests. Large car park with space for cars, vans, and tour buses.

### Q: How do I get to Aquaverse from the airports?
- From Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK): 1.5-2 hours by car/taxi, THB 1,500-2,000
- From U-Tapao Airport (UTP): 20-30 minutes, THB 400-600 (closest airport)
- From Don Mueang Airport (DMK): 2-2.5 hours, THB 1,800-2,500

## What to Bring & Wear

### Q: What should I wear to Aquaverse?
- **Allowed:** Swimsuits, bikinis, board shorts, rash guards, lycra activewear
- **Not allowed on slides:** Clothing with buttons, zippers, or metal buckles; denim; loose jewelry
- **Recommended:** Water shoes for walking between zones; cover-ups for restaurant areas
- All guests must shower before entering water attractions.

### Q: Can I bring my own towel to Aquaverse?
Yes, bring your own towel to save money. Towel rental is available at the park for an additional fee. A quick-dry microfiber towel is ideal.

### Q: Can I bring food and drinks into Aquaverse?
No, outside food and beverages are not allowed. Exceptions: baby food, baby formula, medically necessary dietary items, and sealed water bottles. The park has multiple restaurants with food priced at THB 80-350 per item.

### Q: How big are the lockers at Aquaverse?
Large enough to fit a backpack, change of clothes, shoes, and personal items. Located near the entrance, accessible unlimited times via RFID wristband. Free with online tickets or THB 300 at the gate.

### Q: What items are prohibited at Aquaverse?
Outside food and drinks, glass containers, skateboards/scooters/hoverboards, folding chairs and pop-up tents, inflatable toys and beach balls, sharp objects, drones, and professional photography/video equipment. GoPros and selfie sticks are not allowed on rides but fine in walking areas.

## Rides & Attractions

### Q: How many zones does Aquaverse have?
9 movie-themed zones: Hotel Transylvania (kids), Ghostbusters (thrill), Zombieland (extreme), Jumanji (adventure), Surf's Up (surfing), Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs (lazy river), Bad Boys Race Club (go-karts), The Emoji Movie (mini golf), and the Mega Wave Pool Omnitheatre. Seven zones are water-based and included in general admission. Two zones (go-karts and mini golf) are dry activities with an extra fee.

### Q: What is the scariest ride at Aquaverse?
"The Beast" in the Zombieland zone features an 18-meter vertical drop where the floor drops out from under you. The zone also has a 12-meter free-fall drop slide and a 360-degree spin slide. Minimum height: 120 cm.

### Q: What are the height restrictions for rides?
- Zombieland rides: 120 cm minimum
- FlowRider surf simulator: 110 cm minimum
- Hotel Transylvania kids zone: No height restriction
- Wave pool & lazy river: No height restriction

### Q: What attractions cost extra at Aquaverse?
Bad Boys Race Club (go-karts and drift bikes) and The Emoji Movie (mini golf) require additional fees. VIP Cabanas are also extra: Deluxe THB 2,500, Super Deluxe THB 3,000, VIP THB 3,500.

### Q: What is the World's First Water Dome?
The Proton Stream Water Dome in the Ghostbusters zone. Riders board themed floats and race through dark, twisting tunnels with light effects before plunging into an enclosed dome of water. Unique to Aquaverse.

### Q: Does Aquaverse have a lazy river?
Yes. The Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs zone features the Swallow Falls River Adventure, a themed lazy river. Float tubes available. One lap takes about 15-20 minutes.

### Q: Can I ride alone (single rider) at Aquaverse?
Most slides can be ridden solo. Some raft rides require 2-4 riders -- single riders are grouped with other guests. The FlowRider is a solo experience.

### Q: What is the age limit for go-karts at Aquaverse?
Height and age restrictions apply. Go-karts cost THB 640 as an add-on (not included in standard admission).

### Q: How difficult is the FlowRider at Aquaverse?
Two modes: bodyboarding (beginner-friendly, most guests can do it first try) and stand-up surfing (advanced, requires practice). Instructors available. Minimum height: 110 cm.

### Q: Which zones are best for young kids?
Hotel Transylvania (SE Asia's largest kids water playground), Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs (gentle lazy river), Mega Wave Pool (shallow areas), and The Emoji Movie (mini golf).

### Q: Are there non-water activities at Aquaverse?
Yes: Bad Boys Go-Karts, Emoji Movie Mini Golf, onsen spa, live shows at the Omnitheatre, character meet-and-greets, and themed gift shops.

## Food & Dining

### Q: What restaurants are inside Aquaverse?
The park uses a credit band system called Foodville. Options include: food court (burgers, grilled meats, Thai dishes, THB 80-350), Thai food stalls (rice bowls, fried chicken, THB 80-350), bar area near wave pool (smoothies, juices, cocktails, THB 80-200), and snack carts throughout the park.

### Q: Is halal food available at Aquaverse?
No dedicated halal-certified restaurant, but several suitable options including seafood, grilled chicken, and vegetable dishes. Pork items are clearly marked.

### Q: Are there vegetarian or vegan options?
Yes, vegetarian options available at most food outlets including veggie burgers, salads, rice dishes, and Thai vegetable stir-fries. Vegan options more limited but available upon request.

### Q: How does the RFID payment system work?
Guests receive an RFID wristband at entry. Load credit at top-up stations using cash or credit card. Tap to pay at all food stalls, shops, and activities. Unused balance is refunded at exit kiosks.

## Facilities & Services

### Q: Is there free WiFi at Aquaverse?
Yes, free WiFi is available but can be slow during peak hours. Consider using mobile data for reliable connectivity.

### Q: Is there a first aid station at Aquaverse?
Yes, fully staffed first aid station near the main entrance. Lifeguards stationed at all water attractions.

### Q: What happens if I lose something at Aquaverse?
Report to the Lost and Found desk near the main entrance. For post-visit inquiries, call +66 (0) 33 004 999. Use lockers (free with online tickets) for valuables.

### Q: Is Aquaverse accessible for guests with disabilities?
Accessible pathways and ramps throughout most areas. Accessible restrooms available. Some water rides have physical requirements that limit accessibility. Contact the park in advance for specific questions.

### Q: Can I re-enter the park after leaving?
Re-entry is generally not permitted. Speak with guest services if you need to retrieve something from your car.

### Q: Is Aquaverse suitable for elderly visitors or seniors?
Yes. Seniors can enjoy the lazy river, wave pool viewing areas, live shows, restaurants, and shaded rest areas. Cabana rentals from THB 2,500 provide a comfortable base.

### Q: How does Aquaverse compare to Ramayana Water Park?
Aquaverse features Hollywood movie-themed zones and unique attractions like go-karts and FlowRider surfing. Ramayana is larger with more slides and a natural Thai-garden setting. Aquaverse is better for families and movie fans; Ramayana suits thrill-seekers.

### Q: Is Aquaverse the same as Cartoon Network Amazone?
Yes. Originally Cartoon Network Amazone (opened 2014). Rebranded to Columbia Pictures Aquaverse in 2021 with all-new movie-themed zones. Same location, completely different theming and branding.

### Q: Can I visit Aquaverse as a solo traveler?
Absolutely. FlowRider, wave pool, lazy river, and go-karts are all enjoyable alone. Staff are friendly and the park is safe.

### Q: Is Aquaverse good for a couple's date?
Yes. Couples enjoy the FlowRider challenge, lazy river, go-kart racing, and themed photo spots. Consider upgrading to a cabana for a more private experience.
