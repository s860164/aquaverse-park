# All Aquaverse Attractions & Rides

> Explore all 9 movie-themed zones, 20+ rides and attractions, plus go-karts, mini golf, onsen, and VIP cabanas at Columbia Pictures Aquaverse in Pattaya.

## Key Facts

- **Total Zones:** 9 movie-themed zones -- 7 water zones + go-karts + mini golf
- **Top Rides:** World's First Water Dome (Ghostbusters), The Beast 18m drop (Zombieland), FlowRider (Surf's Up)
- **Best for Kids:** Hotel Transylvania Zone -- SE Asia's largest branded aqua playground
- **Best for Thrills:** Zombieland & Ghostbusters zones for adrenaline junkies
- **Extra Fee:** Go-karts (Bad Boys) and mini golf (Emoji Movie) cost extra; everything else included in general admission
- **Also:** Onsen & steam room, VIP cabanas (from THB 2,500), RFID wristband photo system

## Zone 1: Hotel Transylvania

**Southeast Asia's Largest Branded Aqua Playground**

The Hotel Transylvania zone is paradise for young visitors and families, featuring an incredible array of water-based fun designed specifically for children of all ages. Fully themed around the Hotel Transylvania movie franchise.

### Key Attractions
- **9 Kid-Friendly Water Slides** -- Gentle slides for children of varying ages and confidence levels
- **Dozens of Water Features** -- Splash pads, water cannons, sprinklers, tipping buckets, interactive play elements
- **2 of Thailand's Largest Water Dump Buckets** -- Giant buckets that dump hundreds of gallons on waiting guests
- **Foam Parties** -- Scheduled throughout the day
- **Character Meet-and-Greets** -- Meet Drac, Mavis, Blobby, and other characters
- **Water Rockets** -- Shoot water rockets into the sky

**Height Restriction:** None -- all ages welcome
**Included in ticket:** Yes

## Zone 2: Ghostbusters

**Home to the World's First Water Dome**

The Ghostbusters zone houses the World's First Water Dome, known as the "Proton Stream" -- a completely unique water ride concept exclusive to Aquaverse. Complete with the famous Firehouse entrance.

### Key Attractions
- **Proton Stream (World's First Water Dome)** -- Riders race through dark, twisting tunnels on Ghostbusters floats before plunging into an enclosed dome of water. One-of-a-kind experience
- **Ghost Trap Adventure** -- Interactive dark ride experience
- **Slimer Speed Racer Slides** -- High-speed water slides
- **Asia's First Ghostbusters Supernatural Water Slide** -- Exclusive to this park
- **Ghostbusters Floats in the Dark** -- Ride through dark tunnels with light effects
- **Iconic Firehouse Entrance** -- Recreation of the Ghostbusters headquarters

**Thrill Level:** High
**Included in ticket:** Yes
**Tip:** Ride the Water Dome first thing in the morning to avoid long queues.

## Zone 3: Zombieland

**Home to Asia's Most Extreme Water Slides**

The Zombieland zone is where adrenaline junkies find their paradise. Home to "The Beast" -- one of Asia's tallest water slides with a heart-stopping 18-meter vertical drop.

### Key Attractions
- **"The Beast" (18-Meter Vertical Drop)** -- Stand on a trapdoor platform, the floor drops out beneath you, and you plunge nearly straight down
- **12-Meter Free-Fall Drop Slide** -- Steep body slide with stomach-dropping free-fall
- **360-Degree Spin Slide** -- Fully enclosed tube slide with a complete 360-degree loop
- **Body Slide** -- Classic high-speed body slide with steep drops and sharp turns
- **Mat Racer** -- Multi-lane mat slides for racing friends and family
- **Zombie Chase** -- Themed experience navigating a zombie-infested course

**Height Restriction:** Most slides require minimum 120 cm (3'11")
**Thrill Level:** Extreme
**Included in ticket:** Yes

## Zone 4: Jumanji

**Enter the Wild Jungle Adventure**

The Jumanji zone brings the adventure film franchise to life with lush jungle scenery, roaring water features, and adrenaline-pumping slides. Great for teens, adults, and adventurous older kids.

### Key Attractions
- **Jaguar Mountain** -- Centerpiece ride. Climb the jungle mountain and descend through winding slides surrounded by waterfalls
- **Viper Slide** -- Twisting, turning enclosed tube slide mimicking snake movement
- **Jungle Stampede** -- Multi-person raft ride through rapids
- **Mandrill Chase Splash Pool** -- Splash pool with animatronic mandrills at the bottom

**Thrill Level:** Medium-High
**Included in ticket:** Yes
**Tip:** The raft ride is best with a full group. Solo riders are grouped with others.

---

## Zone 5: Surf's Up

**FlowRider Surf Simulator with Pro Instruction**

Ride the 40-foot FlowRider Double surf simulator with professional instruction. Two modes: bodyboarding (beginner-friendly) and stand-up surfing (advanced).

### Key Attractions
- **40-Foot FlowRider Double Surf Machine** -- State-of-the-art double surf simulator
- **Professional Surf Instruction** -- Certified instructors for beginners
- **Boogie Boarding** -- Great option for first-timers

**Height Restriction:** 110 cm minimum
**Thrill Level:** Medium
**Extra Fee:** Yes -- pay at the zone using RFID wristband
**Tip:** Go right when the park opens to avoid afternoon queues.

---

## Zone 6: Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs

**Swallow Falls River Adventure & Lazy River**

A gentle, meandering 335-meter lazy river taking you through scenes inspired by the animated film, surrounded by giant food sculptures and wacky Foodimals.

### Key Attractions
- **Swallow Falls River Adventure (335 meters)** -- Themed lazy river winding through food-inspired scenery. Grab a float tube and drift
- **Wacky Foodimals** -- Colorful animatronic food-animal hybrids for photo opportunities
- **Food-Themed Experiences** -- Interactive water features shaped like oversized food items

**Height Restriction:** None -- all ages
**Thrill Level:** Low (relaxing)
**Included in ticket:** Yes
**Tip:** Great mid-day break. One lap takes about 15-20 minutes. Go around as many times as you like.

## Zone 7: Bad Boys Race Club

**Miami-Themed Go-Kart Racing Circuit**

High-speed go-kart racing on a Miami-inspired 500-meter circuit. One of the non-water attractions that makes Aquaverse a full entertainment destination.

### Key Attractions
- **500-Meter Miami-Themed Go-Kart Circuit** -- Custom-designed track with twists, turns, and straightaways
- **Custom Go-Karts (Up to 80 km/h)** -- Purpose-built karts with adjustable settings
- **Drift Bikes** -- Specialized bikes for controlled drifting

**Extra Fee:** Yes -- not included in general admission. Pay using RFID wristband.
**Tip:** Great dry activity while digesting lunch.

## Zone 8: The Emoji Movie

**Creative Mini Golf with Prizes**

A colorful 9-hole emoji-themed mini golf course with creative obstacles. Compete for prizes.

### Key Attractions
- **9-Hole Emoji-Themed Mini Golf Course** -- Unique obstacles with emoji characters and phone-world theming
- **Creative Obstacles** -- Each hole features different themed challenges
- **Prize System** -- Score well and win prizes

## Zone 9: Mega Wave Pool Omnitheatre

**Waves, Films, Concerts & Foam Parties**

The beating heart of Aquaverse. Massive wave pool with mega waves every 20 minutes, doubling as an outdoor entertainment venue.

### Key Attractions
- **Mega Wave Pool** -- Enormous pool generating large waves every 20 minutes
- **Foam Parties** -- Scheduled foam blasts in the wave pool area
- **Outdoor Movie Screenings** -- Watch movies on giant LED screens while floating
- **Live Concerts & DJ Sets** -- Regular performances, especially weekends and holidays
- **Dolby DTS Surround Sound** -- Cinema-quality audio throughout
- **Giant LED Screens** -- Multiple high-definition screens visible from anywhere in the pool

**Height Restriction:** None (shallow areas for young children)
**Included in ticket:** Yes
**Tip:** Most popular area, especially afternoons. Grab your float early. Check schedule for foam party and movie times.

## Additional Attractions & Services

### Onsen & Steam Room
Japanese-style hot spring bath and steam room. A unique feature that sets Aquaverse apart from other water parks. Great for relaxing tired muscles after a day of water slides. Included in ticket.

### VIP Cabanas
Three tiers available:
- **Deluxe Cabana** -- THB 2,500/day -- Shaded area, seating, locker, towels
- **Super Deluxe Cabana** -- THB 3,000/day -- Larger space, premium seating, drinks included
- **VIP Cabana** -- THB 3,500/day -- Best location, food & drink credits, priority service

### RFID Wristband Photo & Video System
Every guest receives an RFID wristband that works as a cashless payment system and connects to automatic ride photo/video cameras. View and purchase all your photos at kiosks or the exit.

### Height Restrictions Summary

| Height | Accessible Attractions |
|---|---|
| Under 100 cm | Hotel Transylvania, lazy river, wave pool (with life jacket) |
| 100-120 cm | Most Jumanji rides, some Ghostbusters slides, Surf's Up (with instructor) |
| 120 cm+ | All rides including Zombieland extreme slides |
| All ages | Lazy river, wave pool, onsen, mini golf, character meet-and-greets |

Free life jackets available for younger guests and non-swimmers.

## FAQ

### Q: How many zones does Aquaverse have?
9 movie-themed zones. Seven are water-based and included in general admission. Two (go-karts and mini golf) are dry activities with an extra fee.

### Q: What is the scariest ride at Aquaverse?
"The Beast" in Zombieland -- an 18-meter vertical drop where the floor drops out beneath you. Minimum height: 120 cm.

### Q: Are there height restrictions at Aquaverse?
Yes. Zombieland and Ghostbusters thrill rides require 120 cm minimum. Hotel Transylvania has no restrictions. Height is checked at each ride entrance.

### Q: What attractions cost extra?
Bad Boys Race Club (go-karts), The Emoji Movie (mini golf), and VIP cabanas.

### Q: What is the World's First Water Dome?
The Proton Stream in the Ghostbusters zone. Riders race through dark tunnels on floats before plunging into an enclosed dome of water. Unique to Aquaverse.

### Q: Does Aquaverse have a lazy river?
Yes. The Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs zone features a 335-meter themed lazy river. One lap takes 15-20 minutes.
