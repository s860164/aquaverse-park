The park operates daily from 10 AM to 6 PM, except Wednesdays when it is closed for maintenance.

Adult Ticket Prices Explained

Any visitor taller than 120 cm is classified as an adult at Aquaverse. The standard adult admission grants full-day access to all 9 themed zones, all water slides, rides, go-karts at Bad Boys Raceway, mini golf at the Emoji Movie Fun Zone, character shows, and live entertainment. Here is what you need to know about adult pricing:

  • Walk-in / gate price: THB 1,595 per person. This is the full price you pay if you show up without a pre-booked ticket.
  • Online booking price: From THB 870 per person. This is the same ticket with the same access, but purchased through the official online booking channel before your visit.
  • The saving: THB 725 per adult (45% off). For a couple, that is THB 1,450 saved in under two minutes of booking time.

All adult tickets — whether purchased at the gate or online — include full access to every attraction in the park. There are no restricted zones or "premium ride" upcharges beyond the base ticket. The only difference between gate and online tickets is the price you pay and the free locker that comes with online bookings.

Child & Toddler Ticket Pricing

Aquaverse uses height rather than age to determine ticket categories. This is standard practice for theme parks in Thailand and worldwide. Height is measured at the entrance gate when you arrive.

  • Under 90 cm (toddlers): Free entry. No ticket required. Must be accompanied by a ticketed adult.
  • 90 cm to 120 cm (children): THB 1,295 at the gate or from THB 690 online — a 47% saving.
  • Over 120 cm: Classified as adult, pays adult pricing regardless of age.

The Hotel Transylvania zone is specifically designed for younger visitors and is a major reason families with children love Aquaverse. It features Southeast Asia's largest branded kids' water playground, 9 child-friendly slides, over 100 water play features, and regular character meet-and-greets with Dracula, Mavis, and Blobby. All of this is included in the standard child ticket.

VIP Experience Package

For visitors who want the ultimate premium day out, Aquaverse offers a VIP Experience package starting from THB 2,500 per person when booked online. This premium tier transforms your visit from a standard theme park day into a luxury experience. Here is what the VIP package includes:

  • Private cabana rental: A shaded cabana with comfortable seating for your group, complete with a secure storage area and power outlets to charge devices.
  • Fast-pass access: Skip the regular queue on all major rides and slides. During peak periods this alone can save you hours of waiting.
  • Welcome drink: A complimentary beverage upon arrival.
  • Premium locker: An upgraded locker in a convenient location, included at no extra cost.
  • Premium towel set: No need to bring your own towels.
  • Dedicated VIP concierge: A staff member available to assist with reservations, directions, and any requests throughout your visit.

The VIP Experience is particularly worthwhile during Thai school holidays, weekends, and the December–January peak season when ride queues can stretch to 30–45 minutes. The fast-pass access alone pays for the price difference when you calculate how many extra rides you will fit in. Groups of 4 or more should inquire about VIP group packages for additional savings.

Add-Ons, Lockers & RFID Wristbands

Beyond the base ticket, there are a few additional costs to be aware of when budgeting for your Aquaverse visit:

Locker Rental

Lockers come in two sizes: standard (THB 100) and large (THB 200) when purchased at the gate. However, online tickets include a free locker rental, which is another reason to book in advance. You will want a locker to store your phone, wallet, keys, and any bags while you enjoy the rides.

RFID Wristband (Cashless Payment System)

Aquaverse operates a fully cashless payment system inside the park using mandatory RFID wristbands. When you enter, you pay a THB 200 refundable deposit for the wristband. You then load credit onto it (in any amount) and use it to tap-and-pay at every food stall, restaurant, souvenir shop, and extra service point inside the park. When you leave, return the wristband at the exit to receive your THB 200 deposit back plus any unspent credit. This system means you do not need to carry cash or worry about wet currency on the rides.

Free Parking

All ticket holders receive free parking regardless of ticket type. The car park is large and well-organized. If you are driving to Aquaverse or arriving by private transfer, you will not pay any parking fees.

Food & Drinks

Food prices inside Aquaverse are reasonable by theme park standards. Budget approximately THB 200–400 per person for lunch and snacks. There are 5+ themed restaurants serving Thai and international cuisine. For a complete guide to optimizing your in-park spending, see our visitor tips page.

How to Book Aquaverse Tickets (Step-by-Step)

Booking Aquaverse tickets online takes less than 3 minutes and saves you hundreds of baht per person. Here is the process:

  1. Choose your visit date: Remember that Aquaverse is closed every Wednesday. Pick any other day of the week. The park operates 10 AM to 6 PM daily.
  2. Select ticket types: Choose the number of adult tickets (visitors over 120 cm), child tickets (90–120 cm), and any VIP upgrades. Toddlers under 90 cm are free and do not need a ticket.
  3. Complete payment: Pay securely with credit card, debit card, or other accepted payment methods. You will receive a confirmation email immediately.
  4. Save your e-ticket: Download or screenshot your booking confirmation. You will need to show the QR code or booking reference at the entrance.
  5. Arrive at the park: Head to the online ticket redemption counter (separate from the walk-in queue, so it is faster). Show your e-ticket, receive your RFID wristband, and you are in.

Pro tip: Book at least 1 day in advance for the best rates. Same-day online bookings are usually available but occasionally carry a slightly higher price than advance bookings.

8 Money-Saving Tips for Aquaverse

Maximizing value at Aquaverse goes beyond just booking online. Here are our tried-and-tested strategies for getting the most out of every baht:

  1. Always book online: This is the single biggest saving. At 45% off the gate price for adults and 47% off for children, online booking is non-negotiable. There is simply no scenario where paying the walk-in price makes sense.
  2. Visit on weekdays: Weekday visits (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday) mean shorter queues, more ride repetitions, and a more relaxed experience. Some online platforms also offer weekday-specific pricing that is lower than weekend rates.
  3. Book 7+ days in advance: Early-bird pricing is occasionally available for bookings made a week or more ahead. This can stack an additional 5–10% on top of the standard online discount.
  4. Follow Aquaverse on social media: Flash sales, promo codes, and limited-time offers are frequently posted on the park's official Facebook and Instagram pages. These codes can save an extra THB 50–200 per ticket.
  5. Bring your own sunscreen and waterproof phone pouch: These items are marked up significantly inside the park. A THB 30 waterproof pouch from a convenience store does the same job as the THB 150 version sold inside the gates.
  6. Use the free locker from your online booking: Do not pay for an extra locker at the gate. One locker per group is usually enough if you consolidate valuables.
  7. Load your RFID wristband conservatively: Start with THB 300–500 per person. You can always top up inside the park, but unspent credit means a longer queue at the refund counter when you leave.
  8. Arrive at opening (10 AM): The first 1–2 hours are the least crowded. Hit the most popular rides first — especially The Beast and the Ghostbusters slides — before queues build up after noon. This is the best visitor tip we can give you for maximizing your day.

Seasonal Pricing & Best Times to Visit

Aquaverse ticket prices remain relatively consistent throughout the year, but the value you get fluctuates based on crowd levels and occasional seasonal promotions:

Peak Season (Higher Crowds, Standard Pricing)

  • December – January: Christmas and New Year period. Highest crowds of the year, especially Christmas week. Standard online pricing applies — book well in advance as popular dates can sell out.
  • Thai school holidays (March – May): Songkran in April is an especially busy time. However, the park sometimes runs Songkran water festival promotions with special activities and occasional ticket discounts.
  • Chinese New Year (January/February): A 3–5 day peak with many visitors from China. Book ahead.

Shoulder Season (Moderate Crowds, Best Deals)

  • June – July: International summer visitors increase crowds slightly, but it is not as packed as December. Good balance of availability and weather.
  • November: Loy Krathong period can bring promotional pricing. The weather is excellent with lower humidity and cooler temperatures making outdoor activities very comfortable.

Low Season (Smallest Crowds, Potential Discounts)

  • August – October: Monsoon season means occasional afternoon rain showers, but the park stays open. This is when you are most likely to find additional promotions on top of the standard online discount. Crowds are thinnest and you may ride The Beast 5 times without a significant queue.
  • February: After Chinese New Year and before Songkran. A quieter window with comfortable weather.

Regardless of when you visit, the standard online discount of up to 45% is always available. Seasonal promotions stack on top of this for even deeper savings at certain times of year.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much are Aquaverse tickets in 2026?

Aquaverse gate prices are THB 1,595 per adult and THB 1,295 per child (under 120 cm). However, booking online saves up to 45%, with adult tickets available from THB 870 and child tickets from THB 690. Online tickets also include a free locker rental worth THB 100–200, making the total saving even greater. Visit our tickets page for the latest pricing.

Is it cheaper to buy Aquaverse tickets online or at the gate?

Online tickets are significantly cheaper. Adult tickets start at THB 870 online versus THB 1,595 at the gate — a saving of THB 725 per person (45%). There is no advantage to buying at the gate. Online tickets also include a free locker rental, which costs THB 100–200 extra when purchased at the gate. We always recommend booking through the official online channels.

Are there Aquaverse promo codes or discount codes?

Aquaverse occasionally releases promo codes through their official social media channels (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok) and email newsletter. The best ongoing discount is simply booking through the official online channels, which already offers up to 45% off. Seasonal promotions during Songkran (April), Thai school holidays, and the December holiday period typically offer additional savings of 5–10%. Follow the park's social media accounts for flash sales.

Do kids under a certain age get free entry to Aquaverse?

Aquaverse uses height rather than age to determine ticket pricing. Children under 90 cm tall enter for free. Children between 90 cm and 120 cm require a child ticket (THB 1,295 at the gate, from THB 690 online). Visitors taller than 120 cm pay the adult price. Height is measured at the entrance gates. For a complete guide to visiting with children, see our Aquaverse with kids guide.

What is included in the Aquaverse VIP Experience ticket?

The Aquaverse VIP Experience (from THB 2,500 online) includes a private cabana rental for the day, fast-pass access to skip ride queues on all major attractions, a welcome drink, complimentary premium locker, premium towel set, and a dedicated VIP concierge. It is ideal for guests who want maximum comfort and minimal waiting, especially during peak periods when queues for popular rides like The Beast can exceed 30 minutes.

What is the RFID wristband at Aquaverse and does it cost extra?

Aquaverse uses a mandatory RFID wristband system for all cashless payments inside the park. You pay a THB 200 refundable deposit when you arrive. Load credit onto the wristband at top-up stations to buy food, drinks, souvenirs, and extras throughout your visit. Any unused balance and the THB 200 deposit are fully refunded when you return the wristband at the exit counters. It is a convenient system that means you do not need to carry cash on the rides.

Can I get a refund if it rains at Aquaverse?

Aquaverse does not typically offer rain refunds because the park remains open during rain — and frankly, riding water slides in warm tropical rain is part of the fun. However, online tickets purchased through official channels can usually be rescheduled for a different date within 30 days if you contact customer support before your visit date. Always check the specific refund and rescheduling policy displayed during your booking process.

Is parking free at Aquaverse?

Yes, parking is completely free at Aquaverse for all ticket holders. The park has a spacious parking area that accommodates cars, motorbikes, and tour buses. There is no need to budget for parking costs regardless of your ticket type.
