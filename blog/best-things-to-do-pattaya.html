Pattaya at a Glance

Pattaya is one of Thailand's most versatile tourist destinations, and the sheer variety of things to do here consistently surprises first-time visitors. Located just 150 kilometers southeast of Bangkok on the eastern shore of the Gulf of Thailand, this coastal city has reinvented itself from a niche nightlife destination into a well-rounded holiday spot that genuinely caters to families, couples, solo travelers, and seniors alike.

What makes Pattaya unique is the concentration of attractions within a compact area. World-class water parks sit within minutes of ancient wooden temples. Pristine island beaches are a quick boat ride from bustling night markets. Indoor museums neighbor outdoor botanical gardens. You could spend two weeks in Pattaya and still discover something new each day.

This guide covers the 15 best things to do, organized by category to help you plan efficiently. Whether you have one day or one week, whether your budget is shoestring or luxury, you will find plenty to fill your time. Theme Parks

1. Columbia Pictures Aquaverse

Cost: around THB 1,200 online (gate ~THB 1,600, save over 25%) | All-Inclusive: ~THB 1,600 | Duration: Full day | Hours: 10 AM - 6 PM, closed Wed

The undisputed number one attraction in Pattaya. Aquaverse is a movie-themed water park featuring 9 themed zones inspired by Columbia Pictures films including Ghostbusters, Jumanji, Hotel Transylvania, and Bad Boys. With 20+ rides and water attractions across a massive complex, it caters to every age group and thrill level. The lazy river is perfect for relaxation, the wave pool is fun for all ages, and The Beast is one of the most intense water slides in Southeast Asia.

Beyond water rides, Aquaverse also offers go-karts, mini golf, arcade games, and regular character meet-and-greets. The All-Inclusive ticket (~THB 1,600) is excellent value as it covers lunch and snacks throughout the day. Visit on a weekday for shorter queues. The park is located in Na Jomtien, about 20 minutes from central Pattaya. Book tickets online to save over 25% and skip the gate queue.

This park is suitable for all traveler types: families with kids, couples seeking an adventure date, solo travelers, and even seniors who can enjoy the lazy river and themed walkways.

2. Ramayana Water Park

Cost: ~THB 1,190 | Duration: Full day | Hours: 10 AM - 6 PM

Ramayana is Pattaya's second world-class water park, spread across 184,000 square meters with over 50 slides. While Aquaverse wins on theming and entertainment variety, Ramayana excels in sheer slide count and its beautiful Thai-inspired landscaping. The park is built into a hillside, providing impressive drops and speeds.

Ramayana has dedicated kids' zones, an exceptionally long lazy river, a large wave pool, and an aqua play fortress. It is located about 20 minutes east of central Pattaya. For families who have the time, visiting both parks on separate days provides the complete Pattaya water park experience. See our Aquaverse vs Ramayana comparison for a detailed breakdown.

Beaches & Islands

3. Koh Larn (Coral Island)

Cost: THB 30 public ferry or THB 2,000-3,000 private speedboat | Duration: Full day

Koh Larn is the beach destination that Pattaya itself aspires to be. Located just 7 kilometers offshore, this small island offers six main beaches with crystal-clear turquoise water, white sand, and the kind of postcard-perfect scenery that makes Thailand famous. The public ferry from Bali Hai Pier costs just THB 30 and takes 45 minutes, or speedboats arrive in 15 minutes for THB 200-300 per person.

Tawaen Beach is the most popular with full facilities. Samae Beach is quieter with stunning water clarity. Nual Beach (Monkey Beach) is the most secluded. Rent a motorbike on the island (THB 200-300/day) to explore all the beaches at your own pace. Beachfront restaurants serve fresh seafood at local prices. Visit on a weekday for a more peaceful experience.

4. Jomtien Beach

Cost: Free (sunbed rental THB 100-200) | Duration: Half day to full day

Jomtien Beach stretches for 6 kilometers south of Pattaya Beach and offers a distinctly more relaxed atmosphere. The beach is wider, the water is calmer, and the overall vibe is more family-friendly. A flat beachfront promenade is perfect for walking, jogging, or cycling. Beachfront restaurants serve excellent Thai food and fresh seafood. The south end near Aquaverse is particularly peaceful.

Water activities available along Jomtien include banana boat rides, sea kayaks, parasailing, and stand-up paddleboarding. The beach is easily accessible by songthaew from central Pattaya (THB 10) or by Grab (THB 60-100).

5. Wongamat Beach

Cost: Free | Duration: 2-4 hours

Wongamat Beach in North Pattaya is the most upscale beach in the area, lined with luxury hotels including the Centara Grand and Dusit Thani. The beach is cleaner and quieter than Pattaya Beach, with good swimming conditions and attractive coral formations in the water. It is the best beach for couples seeking a premium beach experience without traveling to Koh Larn.

Culture & Temples

6. Sanctuary of Truth

Cost: ~THB 500 | Duration: 2-3 hours

The Sanctuary of Truth (Prasat Satchatham) is one of the most extraordinary structures in all of Southeast Asia. This 105-meter-tall building is constructed entirely from hand-carved teak and ironwood, with every surface covered in intricate carvings depicting Hindu and Buddhist mythology. Under continuous construction since 1981, it blends architecture, art, philosophy, and craftsmanship into a single awe-inspiring experience.

Guided tours run every 30 minutes and provide fascinating context about the mythology represented. The beachfront area beside the Sanctuary offers excellent sunset views. Located in North Pattaya near Wongamat Beach, it pairs naturally with a visit to that beach and the nearby luxury hotels.

7. Nong Nooch Tropical Garden

Cost: ~THB 500 | Duration: 3-5 hours

Nong Nooch is a 600-acre botanical wonderland that far exceeds expectations. The 17-acre French Garden rivals European equivalents with its geometric flower beds and manicured hedges. The butterfly garden houses hundreds of colorful butterflies. A massive cactus greenhouse features unusual specimens from around the world. The dinosaur valley section has life-sized sculptures. Daily cultural shows include traditional Thai dancing.

A tram service operates throughout the vast grounds, making it accessible for visitors of all fitness levels. Located about 15 minutes south of central Pattaya on the road toward Sattahip, it is easy to combine with Aquaverse, which is in the same area.

8. Wat Phra Yai (Big Buddha Temple)

Cost: Free | Duration: 1 hour

The 18-meter golden Buddha statue atop Pratumnak Hill is one of Pattaya's most iconic landmarks, visible from across the bay. Beyond the statue itself, the hilltop viewpoint provides panoramic views of both Pattaya Beach and Jomtien Beach. The temple complex includes smaller shrines, gardens, and merit-making areas. Modest dress is required (cover shoulders and knees). Visit early morning for golden light on the statue and cooler temperatures.

9. Pattaya Floating Market

Cost: ~THB 200 entry | Duration: 1.5-2 hours

Built around a series of lakes connected by wooden walkways, the Pattaya Floating Market combines shopping, eating, and cultural immersion. Vendors sell regional Thai food from boats and waterside stalls representing all four Thai regions. Most dishes cost THB 40-80. Shops offer handmade crafts, Thai silk products, and souvenirs. A boat ride around the market (THB 100-200) provides a relaxing and scenic perspective. Open daily from approximately 9 AM to 8 PM.

Nature & Wildlife

10. Khao Kheow Open Zoo

Cost: ~THB 300 (adults), Night Safari additional | Duration: 4-6 hours

One of the finest zoos in Southeast Asia, Khao Kheow covers over 2,000 acres of natural habitat and houses more than 300 species. Unlike traditional zoos, animals roam in expansive, naturalistic enclosures. Highlights include the giraffe feeding station, the penguin house, the tiger enclosure, and the flamingo lake. Rent a golf cart for maximum flexibility.

The Night Safari is a standout experience: a guided tram ride through the zoo after dark lets you observe nocturnal animals in their natural behaviors. Located about 30 minutes from central Pattaya, the zoo is ideal for families and nature lovers.

11. Khao Chi Chan Buddha Mountain

Cost: Free | Duration: 30 minutes

A 130-meter-tall Buddha image laser-carved into a limestone cliff face, Khao Chi Chan is a unique and impressive sight. The carving was created in 1996 to honor the 50th anniversary of King Bhumibol's accession to the throne. A viewing area with parking means you can appreciate the scale without any hiking or climbing. Located about 20 minutes south of central Pattaya, it is easy to combine with visits to Nong Nooch Garden or Aquaverse.

Indoor Attractions

12. Art in Paradise

Cost: ~THB 400 | Duration: 1.5-2 hours

A 3D art museum where over 150 paintings are specifically designed to be photographed with people as part of the scene. Visitors can pose as if they are riding dinosaurs, falling off cliffs, floating in space, or swimming with sharks. The results are remarkably convincing and genuinely entertaining. Themed zones include underwater, safari, fantasy, Egyptian, and classic art sections. Fully air-conditioned, making it an excellent rainy-day or heat-escape option.

13. Underwater World Pattaya

Cost: ~THB 500 (adults) | Duration: 2-3 hours

Pattaya's aquarium features a 100-meter walk-through tunnel with sharks, rays, sea turtles, and hundreds of tropical fish swimming overhead. A touch pool lets visitors handle starfish and sea cucumbers. Scheduled feeding times throughout the day provide additional entertainment. The moving walkway through the main tunnel means minimal physical effort. Fully air-conditioned and located near Jomtien Beach, it is an ideal half-day activity for all ages.

Shopping & Nightlife

14. Terminal 21 Pattaya

Cost: Free entry (shopping and dining varies) | Duration: 2-4 hours

Terminal 21 is a massive, airport-themed shopping mall where each floor is designed to represent a different world city — Paris, Tokyo, London, Istanbul, San Francisco, and more. The theming is elaborate and photogenic, making it an experience beyond typical mall shopping. The food court on the lower ground floor is legendary, offering virtually every Asian cuisine at genuinely local prices (THB 40-80 per dish). It is the best-value dining in Pattaya's North Road area.

Beyond the food court, the mall houses international fashion brands, a cinema, electronics shops, and Thai designer boutiques. Air-conditioned throughout, Terminal 21 is also the perfect escape from the midday heat. Located on Second Road in North Pattaya, it is easily accessible from anywhere in the city.

15. Walking Street & Night Markets

Cost: Free to walk (food and drinks vary) | Duration: Evening

Walking Street is Pattaya's most famous nightlife strip — a 500-meter pedestrian road that comes alive every evening after 6 PM. Dozens of bars, clubs, live music venues, and restaurants create an electric atmosphere that is worth experiencing at least once, even if nightlife is not your primary interest. The people-watching alone is an experience.

For a more relaxed evening, Thepprasit Night Market (Friday-Sunday evenings) and Jomtien Night Market (nightly) combine cheap and delicious street food, shopping, and live entertainment in a family-friendly setting. Most dishes cost THB 40-80, and the atmosphere is quintessentially Thai. These markets are the best places in Pattaya to eat affordably and experience local life.

Itineraries by Duration

If You Have 1 Day

With just one day, focus on the single activity that most interests you:

  • Water park day: Aquaverse from 10 AM to 5 PM (All-Inclusive ticket, ~THB 1,600). Sunset dinner at Na Jomtien.
  • Culture day: Sanctuary of Truth in the morning, Nong Nooch Garden in the afternoon, night market for dinner.
  • Beach day: Ferry to Koh Larn at 8-9 AM, explore beaches, return by 4 PM, Walking Street in the evening.

If You Have 3 Days

  • Day 1 — Water Park: Aquaverse (full day). Evening dinner at Jomtien Beach.
  • Day 2 — Island & Culture: Morning ferry to Koh Larn (beaches and snorkeling). Afternoon: Sanctuary of Truth (guided tour and sunset). Evening: Walking Street or night market.
  • Day 3 — Explore: Morning: Nong Nooch Garden. Afternoon: Art in Paradise and Underwater World. Evening: Thepprasit Night Market (if weekend) or Jomtien Night Market. Thai massage before departure.

If You Have 5 Days

  • Day 1: Aquaverse water park (full day)
  • Day 2: Koh Larn island day trip (ferry, beaches, snorkeling, seafood lunch)
  • Day 3: Sanctuary of Truth, Nong Nooch Garden, Floating Market
  • Day 4: Khao Kheow Open Zoo (day visit + Night Safari), or Ramayana Water Park
  • Day 5: Jomtien Beach morning, Terminal 21 shopping, Art in Paradise, spa/massage, farewell dinner

Getting Around Pattaya

Pattaya is easy to navigate with multiple transport options at every price point:

  • Songthaew (THB 10): Shared pickup trucks that run along Beach Road and Second Road in a continuous loop. Wave one down and hop on the back. Ring the bell when you want to stop. The cheapest option for short trips within central Pattaya and Jomtien.
  • Grab (THB 60-200): The Southeast Asian equivalent of Uber. Air-conditioned cars with transparent pricing and cashless payment. The best option for comfort, safety (especially at night), and trips to attractions outside the city center.
  • Motorbike taxi (THB 20-80): Fast and cheap for short distances. Riders wear orange vests and gather at intersections. Not recommended for the faint-hearted or those unfamiliar with Thai traffic.
  • Motorbike rental (THB 200-300/day): A popular option for experienced riders. An International Driving Permit is technically required. Always wear a helmet and drive defensively.
  • Private car with driver (THB 1,500-2,500/day): The most comfortable option for day trips or airport transfers. Hotels can arrange this, or book through Grab or local agencies.

From Bangkok: Private car or Grab (THB 1,200-1,500, 90 min), bus from Ekkamai (THB 120-150, 2 hours), direct bus from Suvarnabhumi Airport (THB 140, 2 hours). For complete details, see our getting there guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best things to do in Pattaya?

The top things to do in Pattaya include visiting Aquaverse water park (around THB 1,200 online), exploring the Sanctuary of Truth, taking a boat to Koh Larn island, enjoying Nong Nooch Tropical Garden, shopping at Terminal 21, visiting the Floating Market, trying Thai massage, eating at night markets, and experiencing Pattaya's nightlife. The city offers world-class water parks, beautiful beaches, stunning temples, and incredible food.

How many days do you need in Pattaya?

For a comprehensive Pattaya experience, 3-5 days is ideal. One day for a water park like Aquaverse, one day for the beach and Koh Larn, one day for cultural attractions, and 1-2 additional days for shopping, spa treatments, nightlife, and relaxation. Even 2 days allows you to see the highlights, while a week lets you explore at a relaxed pace.

What are the free things to do in Pattaya?

Free activities in Pattaya include visiting Jomtien Beach, walking along Pattaya Beach, exploring Wat Phra Yai (Big Buddha Temple), viewing Khao Chi Chan Buddha Mountain, hiking Pratumnak Hill for panoramic views, strolling Walking Street, browsing night markets (eating is cheap but not free), and exploring the Silverlake Vineyard viewpoint.

Is Pattaya worth visiting?

Absolutely. Pattaya offers an exceptional variety of activities at outstanding value. World-class water parks, beautiful islands, stunning temples, incredible food, and vibrant nightlife make it one of Thailand's most versatile destinations. It is just 90 minutes from Bangkok, making it extremely accessible. Budget travelers can enjoy Pattaya for under THB 1,500 per day including accommodation and food.

What is the best month to visit Pattaya?

November to February offers the best weather with temperatures of 26-31 degrees Celsius and minimal rain. December and January are peak season with higher prices. March to May is hot season (32-36 degrees), perfect for water parks. June to October is rainy season with lower prices and fewer crowds. Pattaya is a year-round destination as temperatures rarely drop below 25 degrees.

What should I avoid in Pattaya?

Avoid jet ski rental scams (only use reputable operators), gem shops recommended by strangers, unlicensed taxis (use Grab instead), leaving valuables unattended on the beach, drinking tap water, and visiting Aquaverse on Wednesdays (it is closed). Standard travel precautions apply as with any tourist destination.
