How to Get to Aquaverse from Bangkok: All Transport Options (2026)

Updated March 12, 2026 • 7 min read
TL;DR: Fastest: Drive or Grab (~1.5 hours, THB 1,200+). Cheapest: Bus from Ekkamai (THB 108 + THB 350-500 Grab from Pattaya station). Easiest for families: Book a Grab from your hotel door-to-door. From the airport: Grab from Suvarnabhumi takes ~1.5 hours (THB 1,000-1,500).

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse is located in Na Jomtien, Sattahip, on the coast south of Pattaya. The address is 888 Moo 8, Sukhumvit Road, about 150 km southeast of Bangkok (view on Google Maps). Getting there is straightforward with several options to suit every budget and travel style.

Option 1: Drive Yourself

Time: 1.5-2 hours  |  Cost: ~THB 200-300 in tolls + fuel  |  Comfort: High

Driving is the fastest and most flexible option, especially if you have a rental car or are staying outside central Bangkok.

Step-by-Step Route

  1. Start in Bangkok and head toward the Motorway (Route 7 / Bangkok-Chon Buri Expressway). If coming from Sukhumvit or downtown, take the Chalerm Maha Nakhon Expressway to connect to Route 7.
  2. Follow Route 7 toward Chon Buri / Pattaya. This is a modern, well-maintained toll motorway with clear signage in both Thai and English.
  3. Toll gates: You will pass through 2-3 toll booths. Total toll cost is approximately THB 155-205 depending on your starting point. Cash and Easy Pass (M-Pass) accepted.
  4. Exit at Junction 9 (Sattahip/Na Jomtien) and follow signs toward Sattahip on Route 36.
  5. Turn onto Sukhumvit Road (Route 3) heading south. Aquaverse is on the left side, well-signposted.
  6. Enter the parking lot. Parking is free and there is ample space. The lot fills up on weekends and holidays, so arrive before 10:30 AM for the best spots.

Driving Tips

  • Leave Bangkok before 7:30 AM to avoid outbound traffic on Route 7. The motorway gets congested between 7:30-9:30 AM on weekdays.
  • Weekend traffic toward Pattaya peaks on Friday evenings and Saturday mornings. Saturday morning departures from Bangkok can take 2.5-3 hours.
  • The return trip on Sunday evening is notoriously congested. Leave Aquaverse by 3 PM or wait until after 8 PM to avoid the worst traffic.
  • Google Maps and Waze both work well in Thailand. Set your destination to "Columbia Pictures Aquaverse" for accurate navigation.

Option 2: Bus from Ekkamai Bus Terminal

Time: 2-2.5 hours (bus) + 20 min (transfer)  |  Cost: ~THB 460-650 total  |  Comfort: Medium

The public bus is the cheapest way to get from Bangkok to the Pattaya area. You will need a short Grab ride from Pattaya bus station to Aquaverse.

Step-by-Step Directions

  1. Get to Ekkamai BTS station on the Sukhumvit Line (light green line). The bus terminal is directly connected to the BTS station via Exit 2.
  2. Walk to Ekkamai Bus Terminal (Eastern Bus Terminal). It is less than a 2-minute walk from the BTS exit. Follow signs for "Eastern Bus Terminal."
  3. Buy a ticket to Pattaya. Counters are clearly marked. One-way tickets cost THB 108-150 depending on the bus class. First-class buses have air conditioning, assigned seats, and are comfortable for the journey.
  4. Buses depart frequently — approximately every 30-40 minutes from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM. No advance booking needed; just buy and board.
  5. The ride takes approximately 2-2.5 hours depending on traffic. Buses are air-conditioned and have reclining seats.
  6. Arrive at Pattaya Bus Station (North Pattaya Road). From here, open the Grab app and book a ride to "Columbia Pictures Aquaverse." The ride takes about 20-25 minutes and costs THB 350-500.

Alternative: You can also take a songthaew (shared pickup truck taxi) from Pattaya bus station toward Jomtien/Na Jomtien for THB 30-50, but you will need to transfer or walk, making Grab much more practical.

Option 3: Shared Van from Victory Monument

Time: 2-2.5 hours  |  Cost: ~THB 240 per person + THB 350-500 Grab  |  Comfort: Medium

Shared minivans (rot too) are a popular option among locals and budget travelers. They are slightly faster than buses and depart more frequently.

Step-by-Step Directions

  1. Get to Victory Monument BTS station on the Sukhumvit Line. Take Exit 2.
  2. Walk to the minivan departure area. The van station for Pattaya-bound vans is located on the southwest side of Victory Monument (Rang Nam Road side). Ask locals or look for signs reading "Pattaya" in Thai or English.
  3. Buy a ticket at one of the counters. The fare is approximately THB 200-240 per person.
  4. Vans depart when full (typically 10-13 passengers). During busy hours (7-10 AM), you rarely wait more than 15-20 minutes.
  5. The ride takes about 2 hours to central Pattaya. Some vans drop off at different locations; confirm you will be dropped at Pattaya bus station or South Pattaya.
  6. From the drop-off point, take a Grab to Aquaverse (THB 350-500 depending on drop-off location).

Note: Vans are faster than buses but can feel cramped. Luggage space is limited. Not the best option if you have large bags or strollers.

Option 4: Grab or Taxi

Time: 1.5-2 hours  |  Cost: THB 1,200-1,800  |  Comfort: High

Booking a Grab (Southeast Asia's Uber equivalent) is the most convenient door-to-door option and our recommendation for families and groups.

How to Book

  1. Download the Grab app (available on iOS and Android) if you do not already have it.
  2. Set your pickup location to your hotel or accommodation in Bangkok.
  3. Set destination to "Columbia Pictures Aquaverse, Na Jomtien."
  4. Choose your vehicle type. GrabCar is the standard sedan option. GrabCar Plus offers a larger vehicle if you have luggage or children's car seats. Grab 6-seater is available for larger families.
  5. Confirm the price (displayed upfront before booking) and book your ride.

Tips for Grab/Taxi

  • Cost estimate: THB 1,200-1,800 from central Bangkok (Sukhumvit, Silom, Siam). Prices are higher during peak hours and holidays.
  • Tolls: Motorway tolls (THB 155-205) are typically included in the Grab fare, but confirm with your driver.
  • For traditional taxis: If you hail a metered taxi, insist on using the meter plus toll costs. Negotiate a flat rate only if the meter is refused. A fair flat rate from Bangkok center to Aquaverse is THB 1,200-1,500 including tolls.
  • Groups of 3-4: Grab becomes very cost-effective when splitting the fare. At THB 1,500 for 4 people, that is only THB 375 per person — comparable to bus + Grab transfer per person.
  • Return ride: Grab availability near Aquaverse can be limited in the evening. Book your return Grab by 5:00 PM or ask your outbound driver if they can wait (negotiate a round-trip fare).

Option 5: From Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK)

Time: 1-1.5 hours  |  Cost: THB 250-1,500 depending on method

Suvarnabhumi Airport is actually closer to Aquaverse than Bangkok city center, making it a great option if you are heading to the park straight from your flight.

Option A: Grab or Airport Taxi (Recommended)

  1. Clear customs and exit to the arrivals hall.
  2. Book a Grab from the arrivals area (THB 1,000-1,500) or use the official airport taxi counter on the ground floor (Level 1). Airport taxis add a THB 50 airport surcharge plus tolls.
  3. The drive to Aquaverse takes approximately 1-1.5 hours via Route 7.

Option B: Airport Pattaya Bus (Bell Travel Service)

  1. Find the Bell Travel counter on the basement level (Level B1) of Suvarnabhumi Airport, near Gate 8.
  2. Purchase a ticket to Pattaya (approximately THB 250 per person).
  3. Buses depart approximately every 1-2 hours from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
  4. The bus goes to North Pattaya or Jomtien. From there, take a Grab to Aquaverse (THB 350-500, 15-20 minutes).

Tip: If you land early in the morning and plan to spend the day at Aquaverse, the Grab option is worth the extra cost. You can store luggage in park lockers for the day and head to your Pattaya hotel afterward.

Option 6: From Don Mueang Airport (DMK)

Time: 2-2.5 hours  |  Cost: THB 1,500-2,000 by Grab

Don Mueang is Bangkok's budget airline hub, located on the north side of the city. It is further from Aquaverse than Suvarnabhumi, but getting there is still straightforward.

Option A: Grab or Taxi (Recommended)

  1. Book a Grab from the arrivals area. Expect to pay THB 1,500-2,000 depending on traffic.
  2. The drive takes 2-2.5 hours as you need to cross Bangkok and then head southeast on Route 7.

Option B: Train + Bus Combo (Budget)

  1. Take the SRT Red Line train from Don Mueang station to Bang Sue Grand Station (30 minutes, THB 12-42).
  2. Transfer to BTS or MRT to Ekkamai BTS station.
  3. Follow the Ekkamai bus instructions in Option 2 above.
  4. Total cost approximately THB 300-400 per person, but it takes 4-5 hours with all transfers.

Recommendation: Unless you are on a very tight budget, take a Grab from Don Mueang. The train-bus combo is exhausting, especially after a flight.

Option 7: Organized Tour / Day Trip

Time: Full day  |  Cost: THB 1,500-3,500 per person (includes ticket + transport)  |  Comfort: High

Several tour operators offer Aquaverse day trip packages from Bangkok that include round-trip transport and park admission. This is a hassle-free option if you do not want to plan logistics.

What Is Typically Included

  • Hotel pickup and drop-off in Bangkok (usually from major hotel areas like Sukhumvit, Silom, or Siam)
  • Air-conditioned van or minibus transport
  • Aquaverse admission ticket
  • Sometimes includes lunch or a snack pack

Where to Book

  • Klook and KKday are the most popular platforms for booking Aquaverse day trips. Prices typically range from THB 1,500-2,500 per person.
  • GetYourGuide and Viator also list Pattaya water park tours that include Aquaverse.
  • Hotel tour desks in Bangkok can arrange private or group day trips, though prices are usually higher (THB 2,500-3,500).

Tip: Compare the tour package price against booking your own Grab + online ticket. For solo travelers, tours can be cheaper. For families of 4+, booking independently is usually better value.

Getting Back to Bangkok

Planning your return is just as important as getting there. Here are key tips:

  • Book your return Grab early. Grab availability near Aquaverse drops significantly after 5 PM as the park empties. Open the app and book by 4:30-5:00 PM to secure a ride.
  • Negotiate with your outbound driver. If you arrived by taxi, ask your driver if they will wait (or return) for a flat round-trip rate. This is common and eliminates the stress of finding a return ride.
  • Last bus from Pattaya to Bangkok departs around 9:00-11:00 PM from Pattaya Bus Station. If you are taking the bus, leave Aquaverse by 5:30 PM to reach the station in time.
  • Sunday evening traffic warning: The return to Bangkok on Sunday evenings is notorious. Route 7 can back up for hours. Leave by 3 PM or wait until after 8 PM to avoid the worst congestion.
  • Consider staying overnight in Pattaya. If you plan to visit Aquaverse for a full day, staying one night at a nearby hotel and driving back the next morning is a much more relaxed option. Hotels near Aquaverse start from approximately THB 800-1,500 per night.

Combining with Other Pattaya Attractions

If you are making the trip from Bangkok, you might want to combine Aquaverse with other Pattaya attractions to make the most of your journey:

  • Nong Nooch Tropical Garden — Just 10 minutes from Aquaverse. A stunning botanical garden with daily cultural shows. Visit in the morning before heading to the water park.
  • Buddha Mountain (Khao Chi Chan) — An impressive 130-meter golden Buddha carving on a cliff face, 15 minutes from Aquaverse. A quick photo stop that kids enjoy.
  • Jomtien Beach — 20 minutes north of Aquaverse. A popular beach with water sports, restaurants, and a more relaxed vibe than Pattaya Beach.
  • Ramayana Water Park — Pattaya's other major water park, 20 minutes from Aquaverse. If you have two days, visiting both parks is a great plan. See our Aquaverse vs Ramayana comparison to decide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get from Bangkok to Aquaverse?

By car or Grab, the journey takes approximately 1.5 to 2 hours depending on traffic. By bus from Ekkamai, expect around 2 to 2.5 hours for the bus ride plus 20 minutes for the Grab transfer from Pattaya bus station. From Suvarnabhumi Airport, it is about 1 to 1.5 hours by car.

What is the cheapest way to get from Bangkok to Aquaverse?

The cheapest option is the public bus from Ekkamai Bus Terminal, costing around THB 108-150 per person. You will need a short Grab ride from Pattaya bus station to Aquaverse (THB 350-500). Total cost is approximately THB 460-650 per person each way.

Can I take a Grab from Bangkok to Aquaverse?

Yes, and it is our recommended option for families and groups. Grab rides from central Bangkok to Aquaverse typically cost THB 1,200-1,800 depending on traffic and demand. The ride takes about 1.5-2 hours. Split between 3-4 passengers, it becomes very cost-effective at THB 300-600 per person.

Is there a direct bus from Bangkok to Aquaverse?

There is no direct bus to Aquaverse's doorstep. The closest public bus route goes from Ekkamai Bus Terminal in Bangkok to Pattaya Bus Station. From there, you need a Grab or local transport to reach Aquaverse, about 20 minutes south of central Pattaya.

Can I go to Aquaverse directly from Suvarnabhumi Airport?

Yes. The easiest way is to book a Grab or private car from Suvarnabhumi arrivals to Aquaverse. The ride takes about 1-1.5 hours and costs THB 1,000-1,500. A budget alternative is the Bell Travel airport bus to Pattaya (THB 250) plus a Grab to the park.
