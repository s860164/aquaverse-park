Adult Ticket Prices Explained

Any visitor aged 13 or above is classified as an adult at Aquaverse. The standard adult admission grants full-day access to all 9 themed zones, all water slides, rides, mini golf at the Emoji Movie zone, character shows, and live entertainment. Go-karts at Bad Boys Race Club Zone are available as an add-on (THB 990 per 8-minute session at the park, or THB 640 via Klook). Here is what you need to know about adult pricing:

  • Walk-in / gate price: THB 1,595 per person. This is the full price you pay if you show up without a pre-booked ticket.
  • Online booking price: From THB 1,176 per person. This is the same ticket with the same access, but purchased through the official online booking channel before your visit.
  • The saving: THB 424 per adult (27% off). For a couple, that is THB 848 saved in under two minutes of booking time.

All adult tickets — whether purchased at the gate or online — include full access to every attraction in the park. There are no restricted zones or "premium ride" upcharges beyond the base ticket. The only difference between gate and online tickets is the price you pay and the free locker that comes with online bookings.

Child & Toddler Ticket Pricing

Aquaverse uses age-based ticket categories. Age is verified at the entrance gate when you arrive.

  • Children aged 0-2: Free entry. No ticket required. Must be accompanied by a ticketed adult.
  • Children aged 3-12: Child rate applies.
  • Ages 13+: Adult rate applies.

The Hotel Transylvania zone is specifically designed for younger visitors and is a major reason families with children love Aquaverse. It features Southeast Asia's largest branded kids' water playground, 9 child-friendly slides, over 100 water play features, and regular character meet-and-greets with Dracula, Mavis, and Blobby. All of this is included in the standard child ticket.

All-Inclusive Package

For visitors who want the best value and a hassle-free experience, the All-Inclusive Package at THB 1,587 per person is the smartest upgrade. For just THB 409 more than the standard admission, you get several valuable extras bundled in:

  • Full water park access: All 9 themed zones, every water slide, go-karts, mini golf, and all attractions — same as the standard ticket.
  • 200 THB food & drink coupon: A pre-loaded credit for any restaurant or food stall inside the park. This alone covers a full meal.
  • 1 FlowRider surf session: A session on the Surf’s Up FlowRider wave simulator — one of the park’s most popular experiences. Sessions normally require a separate queue and sometimes an additional fee.
  • Locker rental included: No need to pay THB 300 extra for locker storage.
  • Instant e-ticket: Confirmation delivered immediately to your email. Show the QR code at the gate and skip the walk-in queue.

When you add up the value of the food coupon (THB 200), locker rental (THB 300), and surf session, the All-Inclusive Package effectively gives you over THB 400 worth of extras for just THB 409 above the standard price. It is the best deal available for anyone who plans to eat at the park and wants to try the FlowRider.

For groups of 3 or more, the Group Ticket at THB 1,159 per person offers the lowest per-person entry price if you do not need the food coupon and surf session included.

Add-Ons, Lockers & RFID Wristbands

Beyond the base ticket, there are a few additional costs to be aware of when budgeting for your Aquaverse visit:

Locker Rental

Lockers cost THB 300 per day when purchased at the gate. However, online tickets include a free locker rental, which is another reason to book in advance. You will want a locker to store your phone, wallet, keys, and any bags while you enjoy the rides.

RFID Wristband (Cashless Payment System)

Aquaverse operates a fully cashless payment system inside the park using mandatory RFID wristbands. When you enter, you pay a THB 200 refundable deposit for the wristband. You then load credit onto it (in any amount) and use it to tap-and-pay at every food stall, restaurant, souvenir shop, and extra service point inside the park. When you leave, return the wristband at the exit to receive your THB 200 deposit back plus any unspent credit. This system means you do not need to carry cash or worry about wet currency on the rides.

Free Parking

All ticket holders receive free parking regardless of ticket type. The car park is large and well-organized. If you are driving to Aquaverse or arriving by private transfer, you will not pay any parking fees.

Food & Drinks

Food prices inside Aquaverse are reasonable by theme park standards. Budget approximately THB 200–400 per person for lunch and snacks. There are 5+ themed restaurants serving Thai and international cuisine. For a complete guide to optimizing your in-park spending, see our visitor tips page.

How to Book Aquaverse Tickets (Step-by-Step)

Booking Aquaverse tickets online takes less than 3 minutes and saves you hundreds of baht per person. Here is the process:

  1. Choose your visit date: Remember that Aquaverse is closed every Wednesday. Pick any other day of the week. The park operates 10 AM to 6 PM daily.
  2. Select ticket types: Choose the number of adult tickets (visitors aged 13+) and decide whether to go with Standard Admission or the All-Inclusive Package. Groups of 3+ can save even more with the group rate.
  3. Complete payment: Pay securely with credit card, debit card, or other accepted payment methods. You will receive a confirmation email immediately.
  4. Save your e-ticket: Download or screenshot your booking confirmation. You will need to show the QR code or booking reference at the entrance.
  5. Arrive at the park: Head to the online ticket redemption counter (separate from the walk-in queue, so it is faster). Show your e-ticket, receive your RFID wristband, and you are in.

Pro tip: Book at least 1 day in advance for the best rates. Same-day online bookings are usually available but occasionally carry a slightly higher price than advance bookings.

8 Money-Saving Tips for Aquaverse

Maximizing value at Aquaverse goes beyond just booking online. Here are our tried-and-tested strategies for getting the most out of every baht:

  1. Always book online: This is the single biggest saving. At roughly 27% off the gate price, online booking is non-negotiable. There is simply no scenario where paying the walk-in price makes sense.
  2. Visit on weekdays: Weekday visits (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday) mean shorter queues, more ride repetitions, and a more relaxed experience. Some online platforms also offer weekday-specific pricing that is lower than weekend rates.
  3. Book 7+ days in advance: Early-bird pricing is occasionally available for bookings made a week or more ahead. This can stack an additional 5–10% on top of the standard online discount.
  4. Follow Aquaverse on social media: Flash sales, promo codes, and limited-time offers are frequently posted on the park's official Facebook and Instagram pages. These codes can save an extra THB 50–200 per ticket.
  5. Bring your own sunscreen and waterproof phone pouch: These items are marked up significantly inside the park. A THB 30 waterproof pouch from a convenience store does the same job as the THB 150 version sold inside the gates.
  6. Use the free locker from your online booking: Do not pay for an extra locker at the gate. One locker per group is usually enough if you consolidate valuables.
  7. Load your RFID wristband conservatively: Start with THB 300–500 per person. You can always top up inside the park, but unspent credit means a longer queue at the refund counter when you leave.
  8. Arrive at opening (10 AM): The first 1–2 hours are the least crowded. Hit the most popular rides first — especially The Beast and the Ghostbusters slides — before queues build up after noon. This is the best visitor tip we can give you for maximizing your day.

Seasonal Pricing & Best Times to Visit

Aquaverse ticket prices remain relatively consistent throughout the year, but the value you get fluctuates based on crowd levels and occasional seasonal promotions:

Peak Season (Higher Crowds, Standard Pricing)

  • December – January: Christmas and New Year period. Highest crowds of the year, especially Christmas week. Standard online pricing applies — book well in advance as popular dates can sell out.
  • Thai school holidays (March – May): Songkran in April is an especially busy time. However, the park sometimes runs Songkran water festival promotions with special activities and occasional ticket discounts.
  • Chinese New Year (January/February): A 3–5 day peak with many visitors from China. Book ahead.

Shoulder Season (Moderate Crowds, Best Deals)

  • June – July: International summer visitors increase crowds slightly, but it is not as packed as December. Good balance of availability and weather.
  • November: Loy Krathong period can bring promotional pricing. The weather is excellent with lower humidity and cooler temperatures making outdoor activities very comfortable.

Low Season (Smallest Crowds, Potential Discounts)

  • August – October: Monsoon season means occasional afternoon rain showers, but the park stays open. This is when you are most likely to find additional promotions on top of the standard online discount. Crowds are thinnest and you may ride The Beast 5 times without a significant queue.
  • February: After Chinese New Year and before Songkran. A quieter window with comfortable weather.

Regardless of when you visit, the standard online discount of up to 27% is always available. Seasonal promotions stack on top of this for even deeper savings at certain times of year.
