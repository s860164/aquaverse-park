Why Visit Aquaverse?

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse is the world's first fully branded Columbia Pictures theme park, located in the heart of Pattaya's coastal area. Originally opened as Cartoon Network Amazone in 2014, the park was completely redesigned and relaunched in October 2022 with Sony Pictures IP.

    Getting There

    How to Get to Aquaverse

    Located in Na Jomtien, just 1.5 hours from Bangkok and 30 minutes from central Pattaya.

    🚗

    By Car from Bangkok

    1.5 – 2 hours via Motorway Route 7. Free parking at the park. Take exit toward Sattahip and follow signs.

    🚌

    By Bus

    Buses every 30 min from Ekkamai Bus Terminal (BTS Ekkamai). Then take a taxi/Grab (~THB 320-500) from Pattaya bus station.

    🚐

    By Shared Van

    From Victory Monument or Ekkamai, ~THB 240/person. Vans depart hourly to Pattaya.

    🚕

    By Taxi / Grab

    From Bangkok: ~THB 1,200-1,500 + tolls.
    From Pattaya center: ~THB 320-500 (20-30 min drive).

    🚎

    Park Shuttle Bus

    THB 300/person daily shuttle from Pattaya. Book online in advance. Convenient pick-up locations.

    ✈️

    From Airports

    Suvarnabhumi Airport: ~90 min drive.
    U-Tapao Airport (Pattaya): ~25 min drive.

    Aquaverse is open daily from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. The park is closed every Wednesday for scheduled maintenance. Rides close at 5:45 PM, and last admission is at 3:30 PM. Hours may vary during special events and holidays.

    Multiple options available:

    • By shared van: THB 240/person from Victory Monument or Ekkamai
