Kids Favorite

Hotel Transylvania Zone

Southeast Asia's Largest Branded Aqua Playground

The Hotel Transylvania zone is paradise for young visitors and families. It is Southeast Asia's largest branded aqua playground, featuring an incredible array of water-based fun designed specifically for children of all ages. The zone is fully themed around the beloved Hotel Transylvania movie franchise, bringing Drac's spooky (but family-friendly) world to life.

Key Attractions

  • 9 Kid-Friendly Water Slides — Gentle slides designed for children of varying ages and confidence levels
  • Dozens of Water Features — Splash pads, water cannons, sprinklers, tipping buckets, and interactive play elements
  • 2 of Thailand's Largest Water Dump Buckets — Giant buckets that fill up and dump hundreds of gallons on waiting guests below
  • Foam Parties — Scheduled foam parties throughout the day where kids can play in mountains of foam
  • Character Meet-and-Greets — Meet Drac, Mavis, Blobby, and other Hotel Transylvania characters for photos and fun
  • Water Rockets — Shoot water rockets into the sky and watch them splash back down
👶 All Ages Welcome 📸 Character Photos 🎉 Foam Parties 🚀 Water Rockets ✅ Included in Ticket

Parent Tip: This zone has shaded seating areas around the playground where parents can relax while watching their kids play. Arrive early to claim a good spot. For a full family guide, read our Aquaverse with Kids guide.

World's First

Ghostbusters Zone

Home to the World's First Water Dome

The Ghostbusters zone is where Aquaverse truly sets itself apart from every other water park in the world. This zone houses the World's First Water Dome, known as the "Proton Stream" — a completely unique water ride concept you cannot experience anywhere else on the planet. The entire zone is designed around the iconic Ghostbusters franchise, complete with the famous Firehouse entrance.

Key Attractions

  • Proton Stream (World's First Water Dome) — The signature attraction where riders race through dark, twisting tunnels on Ghostbusters floats before plunging into an enclosed dome of water. A one-of-a-kind experience
  • Ghost Trap Adventure — Interactive dark ride experience themed around catching ghosts
  • Slimer Speed Racer Slides — High-speed water slides themed after the iconic green ghost
  • Asia's First Ghostbusters Supernatural Water Slide — A supernatural-themed slide exclusive to this park
  • Ghostbusters Floats in the Dark — Ride special Ghostbusters-themed floats through dark tunnels with light effects
  • Iconic Firehouse Entrance — Walk through a recreation of the Ghostbusters Firehouse headquarters
💥 Thrill Level: High 🔮 World's First Water Dome 👻 Ghost Trap Ride 🌙 Dark Ride Experience ✅ Included in Ticket

Must-Do: The Proton Stream Water Dome is the single most unique attraction at Aquaverse and arguably in all of Southeast Asia. Ride it first thing in the morning to avoid longer queues later in the day. Check our visitor tips for the best ride order strategy.

Extreme

Zombieland Zone

Home to Asia's Most Extreme Water Slides

The Zombieland zone is where adrenaline junkies and thrill seekers will find their paradise. This zone is home to "The Beast" — one of Asia's tallest water slides with a heart-stopping 18-meter vertical drop. Themed around the Zombieland movie franchise, the entire zone has an intense, apocalyptic atmosphere designed to get your heart racing before you even step on a slide.

Key Attractions

  • "The Beast" (18-Meter Vertical Drop) — One of Asia's tallest water slides. Stand on a trapdoor platform, the floor drops out beneath you, and you plunge nearly straight down. Not for the faint of heart
  • 12-Meter Free-Fall Drop Slide — A steep body slide that delivers a stomach-dropping free-fall sensation
  • 360-Degree Spin Slide — A fully enclosed tube slide that spins riders through a complete 360-degree loop
  • Body Slide — A classic high-speed body slide with steep drops and sharp turns
  • Mat Racer — Race friends and family side-by-side on multi-lane mat slides
  • Zombie Chase — A themed experience where you navigate through a zombie-infested course
⚡ Extreme Thrills 📏 18m Vertical Drop 🔄 360° Spin Slide 🏃 Mat Racer ✅ Included in Ticket

Height Restriction: Most slides in the Zombieland zone require riders to be at least 120 cm (3'11") tall. The Beast may have additional restrictions. Height is checked at each ride entrance for safety.

Adventure

Jumanji Zone

Enter the Wild Jungle Adventure

Step into the untamed world of Jumanji, where the jungle comes alive with thrilling water rides and immersive theming. The Jumanji zone brings the adventure film franchise to life with lush jungle scenery, roaring water features, and adrenaline-pumping slides. This zone strikes the perfect balance between excitement and accessibility, making it great for teens, adults, and adventurous older kids.

Key Attractions

  • Jaguar Mountain — The centerpiece ride of the zone. Climb to the top of the jungle mountain and descend through winding slides surrounded by waterfalls and tropical foliage
  • Viper Slide — A twisting, turning enclosed tube slide that mimics the movement of a snake through the jungle
  • Jungle Stampede — A multi-person raft ride that sends groups hurtling down rapids together
  • Mandrill Chase Splash Pool — A splash pool at the bottom where animatronic mandrills "chase" riders into the water
🌿 Immersive Jungle Theme 🏔️ Jaguar Mountain 🐍 Viper Slide 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Great for Groups ✅ Included in Ticket

Tip: The Jungle Stampede raft ride is best enjoyed with a full group. If you are visiting as a couple or solo, you will likely be paired with other guests to fill the raft, which is part of the fun!

Pro Surfing

Surf's Up Zone

FlowRider Surf Simulator with Pro Instruction

Hang ten on the FlowRider surf simulator in the Surf's Up zone! Themed around the animated surfing movie, this zone offers the chance to experience real wave surfing in a completely safe, controlled environment. Whether you are a complete beginner or an experienced surfer, professional instructors are on hand to guide you and help you catch the perfect wave. Note: this attraction requires an additional fee.

Key Attractions

  • 40-Foot FlowRider Double Surf Machine — A state-of-the-art double surf simulator that generates a continuous wave for stand-up surfing and boogie boarding
  • Professional Surf Instruction — Certified instructors will teach you how to balance, catch waves, and ride like a pro
  • Boogie Boarding — A great option for beginners who want to ride the waves while lying or kneeling on a board
🏄 FlowRider 👨‍🏫 Free Pro Instruction 🤙 Boogie Boarding 📷 Great Photo Spot 💰 Additional Fee Required

Extra Fee: The FlowRider surf simulator requires an additional fee beyond general admission. Pay at the zone using your RFID wristband. Try to go right when the park opens to avoid longer afternoon queues.

Relaxing

Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs Zone

Swallow Falls River Adventure & Lazy River

Take a break from the thrills and float along the Swallow Falls River Adventure, Aquaverse's themed lazy river. This gentle, meandering waterway takes you through scenes inspired by the animated film, surrounded by giant food sculptures and the wacky Foodimals from the movie. It is the perfect way to relax, cool down, and recharge between more intense rides.

Key Attractions

  • Swallow Falls River Adventure (335 meters) — A 335-meter themed lazy river that winds through food-inspired scenery from the movie. Grab a float tube and drift at your own pace
  • Wacky Foodimals — Colorful animatronic food-animal hybrids from the movie placed along the river route for photo opportunities
  • Food-Themed Experiences — Interactive water features designed to look like oversized food items that spray and splash riders
🛟 Lazy River 🍔 Food-Themed Scenes 😌 Relaxing Float 👶 All Ages ✅ Included in Ticket

Tip: The lazy river is a great mid-day break. Float tubes are available at the entry point — just grab one and hop in. The circuit takes about 15-20 minutes and you can go around as many times as you like.

Go-Karts · Extra Fee

Bad Boys Race Club

Miami-Themed Go-Kart Racing Circuit

Step off the water slides and onto the race track at the Bad Boys Race Club. Themed around the action-packed Bad Boys franchise, this zone offers high-speed go-kart racing on a Miami-inspired circuit. This is one of the non-water attractions that makes Aquaverse more than just a water park — it is a full entertainment destination.

Key Attractions

  • 500-Meter Miami-Themed Go-Kart Circuit — A 500-meter custom-designed racing track with twists, turns, and straightaways inspired by the streets of Miami
  • Custom Go-Karts (Up to 80 km/h) — Purpose-built karts reaching speeds up to 80 km/h with adjustable settings for different skill levels
  • Drift Bikes — Specialized bikes designed for controlled drifting on a dedicated course
🏎️ Go-Karts 🏁 Miami Theme 🛞 Drift Bikes 💰 Additional Fee Required

Extra Fee: The Bad Boys Race Club is not included in general admission. Go-kart sessions and drift bike rides are charged separately at the venue. You can pay using the credit loaded on your RFID wristband.

Mini Golf · Extra Fee

The Emoji Movie Zone

Creative Mini Golf with Prizes

Take a fun break from the water with a round of mini golf in The Emoji Movie zone. This colorful, emoji-themed mini golf course features creative obstacles and challenges inspired by the world inside your phone. Compete with friends and family for the best score, and top performers can win prizes!

Key Attractions

  • 9-Hole Emoji-Themed Mini Golf Course — A 9-hole mini golf course with unique obstacles decorated with emoji characters and phone-world theming
  • Creative Obstacles — Each hole features different themed challenges that test your putting skills
  • Prize System — Score well enough and you can win prizes at the end of your round
⛳ Mini Golf 🏆 Win Prizes 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 All Ages 💰 Additional Fee Required

Extra Fee: Mini golf in The Emoji Movie zone is not included in general admission and requires an additional fee paid at the venue. Great for a dry break when you need a rest from the water.

Cinema + Waves

Mega Wave Pool Omnitheatre

Waves, Films, Concerts & Foam Parties

The Mega Wave Pool Omnitheatre is the beating heart of Aquaverse. This massive wave pool generates mega waves every 20 minutes, and doubles as an outdoor entertainment venue with giant LED screens and Dolby DTS Surround Sound. Throughout the day (and during special evening events), the Omnitheatre hosts outdoor movie screenings, live concerts, and epic foam parties.

Key Attractions

  • Mega Wave Pool — Enormous pool generating large waves every 20 minutes for body surfing, floating, and splashing
  • Foam Parties — Scheduled foam blasts turn the wave pool area into a massive foam party
  • Outdoor Movie Screenings — Watch movies on giant LED screens while floating in the wave pool
  • Live Concerts & DJ Sets — Regular live music performances and DJ events, especially during weekends and holidays
  • Dolby DTS Surround Sound — Professional-grade audio system that delivers cinema-quality sound throughout the venue
  • Giant LED Screens — Multiple high-definition LED screens visible from anywhere in the wave pool
🌊 Mega Waves Every 20 Min 🎬 Outdoor Cinema 🎸 Live Concerts 🎉 Foam Parties ✅ Included in Ticket

Tip: The wave pool is the most popular area in the park, especially in the afternoon. Grab your float early and stake out a spot. Check the daily schedule board at the entrance for foam party and movie screening times. Evening events during weekends are particularly spectacular.

Additional Attractions & Services

Onsen & Steam Room

Unwind in Aquaverse's Japanese-style onsen (hot spring bath) and steam room. After a full day of water slides and wave pools, there is no better way to relax tired muscles and end your visit on a soothing note. The onsen area is separate from the main water park zones and provides a calmer, more tranquil atmosphere.

This is a unique feature that sets Aquaverse apart from other water parks in the region — few water parks offer a genuine onsen experience alongside their thrill rides.

VIP Cabanas

Upgrade your Aquaverse experience with a private VIP cabana. Enjoy a shaded, exclusive area with comfortable seating, storage for your belongings, and dedicated service. Three tiers are available:

  • Deluxe Cabana — THB 2,500/day — Shaded area, seating, locker, towels
  • Super Deluxe Cabana — THB 3,000/day — Larger space, premium seating, drinks included
  • VIP Cabana — THB 3,500/day — The ultimate package with the best location, food & drink credits, priority service

RFID Wristband Photo & Video System

Every guest at Aquaverse receives an RFID wristband that does more than just grant park access. The wristband works as a cashless payment system (load credit to buy food, drinks, and extras without carrying cash) and also connects to the park's automatic photo and video system.

Cameras positioned at key ride moments automatically capture your reactions on slides and attractions. At the end of the day, you can view and purchase all your photos and videos linked to your wristband — a great way to take home memories of your best moments.

📸 Auto Ride Photos 🎥 Video Capture 💳 Cashless Payment
📏

Height Restrictions

For guest safety, certain water slides and attractions at Aquaverse have minimum height requirements. Height is measured and checked at each ride entrance. Key guidelines:

  • Under 100 cm: Hotel Transylvania zone, lazy river, and wave pool (with life jacket)
  • 100-120 cm: Most Jumanji rides, some Ghostbusters slides, Surf's Up (with instructor)
  • 120 cm+: Required for extreme Zombieland slides (The Beast, 360-degree spin, free-fall drop)
  • All ages: Lazy river, wave pool, onsen, mini golf, character meet-and-greets

Life jackets are available free of charge for younger guests and non-swimmers. Read our family guide for detailed age recommendations by zone.
