From Bangkok

How to Get to Aquaverse from Bangkok

Bangkok is approximately 150 km from Aquaverse. There are several transport options depending on your budget and preference.

🚗

By Car (1.5–2 Hours)

The most flexible option, especially for families. Free parking at the park.

  • Take Motorway Route 7 heading toward Pattaya/Chon Buri
  • Continue past Pattaya and exit toward Sattahip
  • Follow Sukhumvit Road south
  • Look for signs to Columbia Pictures Aquaverse on the left
  • Free parking at the park entrance
Tolls: ~THB 155 each way on Motorway Route 7
Fuel: ~THB 400–600 round trip
Parking: Free
🚌

By Bus (2–2.5 Hours)

The most affordable option from Bangkok. Buses depart frequently from Ekkamai.

  • Go to Ekkamai Bus Terminal (Eastern Bus Terminal)
  • Take BTS to Ekkamai station, Exit 2 — terminal is right there
  • Buses depart to Pattaya every 30 minutes
  • Ticket price: THB 108–150
  • Arrive at Pattaya Bus Terminal, then take Grab/taxi to park
  • Grab/taxi from Pattaya to Aquaverse: THB 350–500
Total cost: ~THB 430–630/person
First bus: 5:00 AM • Last bus: 11:00 PM
🚐

By Shared Van / Minibus (2 Hours)

Faster than buses but slightly more expensive. Comfortable 10-seater minivans.

  • Depart from Victory Monument BTS or Ekkamai
  • Ticket price: THB 240/person
  • Vans depart every hour (more frequently on weekends)
  • Drop-off at Pattaya, then take taxi to park
  • Grab/taxi from Pattaya to Aquaverse: THB 350–500
Total cost: ~THB 560–740/person
Tip: Buy tickets at the van counter, not from touts
🚕

By Taxi / Grab (1.5–2 Hours)

Most convenient option. Door-to-door service with no transfers needed.

  • Cost: THB 1,200–1,500 + tolls (~THB 155)
  • Book via the Grab app for transparent pricing
  • Can be shared with up to 3 passengers
  • Direct door-to-door, no transfers needed
  • Great for groups — split the cost 4 ways = ~THB 340/person
Total cost: ~THB 1,355–1,655/car
Tip: Use Grab for fixed price — avoid meter taxis for long trips
From Airports

Getting to Aquaverse from the Airport

Two airports serve the Pattaya area: Suvarnabhumi (Bangkok's main airport) and U-Tapao (Pattaya's local airport).

✈️

From Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK)

Bangkok's main international airport. Approximately 90 minutes to Aquaverse by car.

  • Taxi: THB 1,500–2,000 (direct to park)
  • Grab: Book via app at arrivals level — similar pricing to taxi
  • Airport Bus + Taxi: Take the airport bus to Pattaya (~THB 200), then taxi to park (THB 350–500)
Distance: ~120 km
Travel time: 1.5 hours (without traffic)
🛩️

From U-Tapao Airport (UTP)

Pattaya's closest airport. Only about 25 minutes to Aquaverse by car.

  • Taxi: THB 400–600 (direct to park)
  • Grab: May have limited availability — pre-book if possible
  • U-Tapao serves flights from Chiang Mai, Kuala Lumpur, and some Chinese cities
Distance: ~25 km
Travel time: 25 minutes

Transport Tips

Getting to Aquaverse from Pattaya

There is no official park shuttle bus. Here are the best ways to reach the park from Pattaya.

Recommended Transport Options

  • Grab: THB 300–500 from Pattaya center (20–30 min). The easiest option — book via the Grab app.
  • Private taxi: Negotiate a fare before you go. Typically THB 300–500 one way.
  • Hotel transfer: Some hotels and tour operators arrange transfers for an extra charge. Ask your hotel reception.
  • Songthaew + Grab: Take a white Songthaew to Na Jomtien (THB 20–50), then Grab the final stretch.
  • Self-drive: Free parking at the park. GPS to “Columbia Pictures Aquaverse” on Google Maps.

Travel Tips

Tips for Getting to Aquaverse

Make your journey smoother with these practical travel tips.

📱

Use the Grab App

Download the Grab app before your trip. It gives you transparent, fixed pricing for rides throughout Thailand — no haggling or meter scams. Works like Uber.

🕐

Avoid Peak Traffic

Avoid traveling Friday evenings (Bangkok → Pattaya) and Sunday evenings (Pattaya → Bangkok). Traffic on Motorway Route 7 can double your journey time on these days.

🌿

Combine with Nearby Attractions

Aquaverse is just 10 minutes from Nong Nooch Tropical Garden, one of Pattaya's top attractions. Consider combining both in a multi-day itinerary.

⏰

Arrive Early

The park opens at 10:00 AM. Arriving by opening time gives you shorter queues and cooler temperatures for the first 2 hours. Aim to depart Bangkok by 7:30–8:00 AM.

🗓️

Check the Day

Aquaverse is closed every Wednesday for maintenance. Don't make the trip without checking! Weekdays (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday) have the smallest crowds.
