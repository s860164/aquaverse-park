# Columbia Pictures Aquaverse Pattaya

> The world's first Columbia Pictures themed water park with 9 movie zones, 20+ rides, and attractions in Pattaya, Thailand.

## Key Facts

- **What:** World's first Columbia Pictures themed water park with 9 movie zones (Ghostbusters, Jumanji, Hotel Transylvania, Zombieland, and more)
- **Where:** Na Jomtien, Sattahip, Pattaya -- 1.5 hours from Bangkok
- **Address:** 888 Moo 8, Sukhumvit Rd, Na Jomtien, Sattahip, Chon Buri 20250, Thailand
- **Hours:** Daily 10:00 AM -- 6:00 PM (closed Wednesdays)
- **Last Admission:** 3:30 PM
- **Rides Close:** 5:45 PM
- **Phone:** +66 (0) 33 004 999
- **Tickets:** From THB 1,176 online (gate price THB 1,595) -- children under 3 free
- **Google Rating:** 4.3/5 (7,840+ reviews)
- **Parking:** Free for all guests
- **Opened:** October 2022 (formerly Cartoon Network Amazone, opened 2014)
- **Best for:** Families, thrill seekers, movie fans -- ideal day trip from Bangkok or Pattaya
- **Highlights:** World's First Water Dome, Asia's tallest water slide (18m), FlowRider surf simulator, go-karts, onsen

## Why Visit Aquaverse?

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse is the world's first fully branded Columbia Pictures theme park, located in the heart of Pattaya's coastal area. Originally opened as Cartoon Network Amazone in 2014, the park was completely redesigned and relaunched in October 2022 with Sony Pictures IP.

- **9 Movie Zones** -- Themed around beloved films including Ghostbusters, Jumanji, Hotel Transylvania, and Zombieland
- **20+ Rides & Attractions** -- From thrilling water slides to a mega wave pool, lazy river, and FlowRider surf simulator
- **Family Friendly** -- SE Asia's largest kids water playground in Hotel Transylvania zone with dozens of water features
- **Beyond Water** -- Go-karts, mini golf, onsen, live shows, and concerts at the wave pool Omnitheatre

## 9 Movie-Themed Zones

### 1. Hotel Transylvania Zone
Southeast Asia's largest branded aqua playground. 9 water slides, dozens of water features, and character meet-and-greets with Drac, Mavis, and Blobby. Foam parties for kids.

### 2. Ghostbusters Zone
Home to the World's First Water Dome. Race through twists and turns in the dark on Ghostbusters floats, then plunge into the massive water dome.

### 3. Zombieland Zone
Features "The Beast" -- one of Asia's tallest water slides with an 18-meter vertical drop. Also includes a 360-degree spin slide and mat racers. Minimum height: 120 cm.

### 4. Jumanji Zone
Jungle-themed adventure zone. Conquer Jaguar Mountain, dodge the Viper slide, and survive the Jungle Stampede raft ride.

### 5. Surf's Up Zone
40-foot FlowRider Double surf simulator with professional instruction. Boogie boarding and stand-up surfing. Additional fee required.

### 6. Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs
Swallow Falls River Adventure -- a 335-meter themed lazy river surrounded by wacky Foodimals and food-themed experiences.

### 7. Bad Boys Race Club
Miami-themed go-kart circuit with custom karts reaching up to 80 km/h and drift bikes. Additional fee required.

### 8. The Emoji Movie Zone
9-hole emoji-themed mini golf course with creative obstacles and prizes. Additional fee required.

### 9. Mega Wave Pool Omnitheatre
The centerpiece of Aquaverse. Mega waves every 20 minutes, foam parties, outdoor movie screenings, live concerts with Dolby DTS surround sound and giant LED screens.

## How to Get There

- **By Car from Bangkok:** 1.5-2 hours via Motorway Route 7. Free parking at the park.
- **By Bus:** Buses every 30 min from Ekkamai Bus Terminal (BTS Ekkamai). Then taxi/Grab (~THB 350-500) from Pattaya bus station.
- **By Shared Van:** From Victory Monument or Ekkamai, ~THB 240/person. Vans depart hourly.
- **By Taxi/Grab:** From Bangkok ~THB 1,200-1,500 + tolls. From Pattaya center ~THB 350-500 (20-30 min).
- **From Airports:** Suvarnabhumi ~90 min drive. U-Tapao (Pattaya) ~25 min drive.
- **No official shuttle bus** from Pattaya. Use Grab or arrange a hotel transfer.

## FAQ

### Q: How much do Aquaverse tickets cost?
Walk-in gate price is THB 1,595 per person. Online advance tickets start from THB 1,176 (~26% off). Children aged 0-2 enter free. Online tickets include locker rental.

### Q: What are the opening hours of Aquaverse?
Aquaverse is open daily from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM (rides close at 5:45 PM). The park is closed every Wednesday for maintenance. Last admission is at 3:30 PM.

### Q: How to get to Aquaverse from Bangkok?
By car: 1.5-2 hours via Motorway Route 7 toward Pattaya. By bus: From Ekkamai Bus Terminal. By shared van: From Victory Monument or Ekkamai, ~THB 240/person. By taxi/Grab: ~THB 1,200-1,500 plus tolls.

### Q: Is Aquaverse closed on Wednesday?
Yes, Columbia Pictures Aquaverse is closed every Wednesday for scheduled maintenance.

### Q: What movies are featured at Aquaverse?
Aquaverse features 9 zones themed around Columbia Pictures movies: Hotel Transylvania (kids zone), Ghostbusters (thrill slides), Jumanji (jungle rides), Zombieland (extreme slides), Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs (lazy river), Surf's Up (FlowRider), Bad Boys (go-karts), The Emoji Movie (mini golf), and the Mega Wave Pool cinema.

### Q: Can you bring food to Aquaverse?
No, outside food and beverages are not allowed. The park has multiple themed dining areas including a food court, Thai food stalls, and snack bars. Baby food and formula are allowed.

### Q: Is Aquaverse good for kids?
Yes. The Hotel Transylvania zone is Southeast Asia's largest branded water playground with 9 kid-friendly slides, dozens of water features, and character meet-and-greets. Children under 3 enter free.
