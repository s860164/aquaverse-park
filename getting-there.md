# How to Get to Aquaverse Pattaya

> Complete transport guide to Columbia Pictures Aquaverse from Bangkok, Pattaya, and the airports -- by car, bus, taxi, or Grab.

## Key Facts

- **Address:** 888 Moo 8, Sukhumvit Rd, Na Jomtien, Sattahip, Chon Buri 20250 ([Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=Columbia+Pictures+Aquaverse+Pattaya))
- **From Bangkok:** 1.5-2 hours by car or taxi / 2-2.5 hours by bus
- **From Pattaya center:** 20-30 minutes by Grab/taxi (THB 350-500)
- **Parking:** Free for all guests -- large, well-lit, and secure
- **No official shuttle:** Use Grab (THB 300-500 from Pattaya) or arrange a private transfer through your hotel or a tour operator
- **Best tip:** Use the [Grab app](https://www.grab.com) for the best taxi prices throughout Thailand
- **Phone:** +66 (0) 33 004 999
- **GPS coordinates:** 12.7644, 100.9478

## Park Location

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse is located along the coastal Sukhumvit Road in the Na Jomtien / Sattahip area, between Pattaya and Sattahip naval base.

## From Bangkok

### By Car (1.5-2 Hours)
The most flexible option, especially for families. Free parking at the park.
- Take **Motorway Route 7** heading toward Pattaya/Chon Buri
- Continue past Pattaya and exit toward **Sattahip**
- Follow **Sukhumvit Road** south
- Look for signs to **Columbia Pictures Aquaverse** on the left
- Free parking at the park entrance
- **Tolls:** ~THB 155 each way on Motorway Route 7
- **Fuel:** ~THB 400-600 round trip

### By Bus (2-2.5 Hours)
The most affordable option from Bangkok.
- Go to **Ekkamai Bus Terminal** (Eastern Bus Terminal)
- Take **BTS to Ekkamai station, Exit 2** -- terminal is right there
- Buses depart to Pattaya **every 30 minutes**
- Ticket price: **THB 108-150**
- Arrive at Pattaya Bus Terminal, then take Grab/taxi to park
- Grab/taxi from Pattaya to Aquaverse: **THB 350-500**
- **Total cost:** ~THB 430-630/person
- **First bus:** 5:00 AM / **Last bus:** 11:00 PM

### By Shared Van / Minibus (2 Hours)
Faster than buses but slightly more expensive. Comfortable 10-seater minivans.
- Depart from **Victory Monument BTS** or **Ekkamai**
- Ticket price: **THB 240/person**
- Vans depart **every hour** (more frequently on weekends)
- Drop-off at Pattaya, then take taxi to park
- Grab/taxi from Pattaya to Aquaverse: **THB 350-500**
- **Total cost:** ~THB 560-740/person
- **Tip:** Buy tickets at the van counter, not from touts

### By Taxi / Grab (1.5-2 Hours)
Most convenient. Door-to-door with no transfers.
- Cost: **THB 1,200-1,500** + tolls (~THB 155)
- Book via the [Grab app](https://www.grab.com) for transparent pricing
- Can be shared with up to 3 passengers
- Great for groups -- split cost 4 ways = ~THB 340/person
- **Total cost:** ~THB 1,355-1,655/car
- **Tip:** Use Grab for fixed price -- avoid meter taxis for long trips

## From Airports

### From Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK)
Bangkok's main international airport. Approximately 90 minutes to Aquaverse.
- **Taxi:** THB 1,500-2,000 (direct to park)
- **Grab:** Book via app at arrivals level -- similar pricing to taxi
- **Airport Bus + Taxi:** Airport bus to Pattaya (~THB 200), then taxi to park (THB 350-500)
- **Distance:** ~120 km
- **Travel time:** 1.5 hours (without traffic)

### From U-Tapao Airport (UTP)
Pattaya's closest airport. Only about 25 minutes to Aquaverse.
- **Taxi:** THB 400-600 (direct to park)
- **Grab:** May have limited availability -- pre-book if possible
- Serves flights from Chiang Mai, Kuala Lumpur, and some Chinese cities
- **Distance:** ~25 km
- **Travel time:** 25 minutes

## From Pattaya Center

### Grab / Taxi
**THB 300-500** one way, 20-30 minutes. Book via the Grab app from Pattaya center. No official park shuttle bus is available. Some hotels and tour operators offer transfers for an extra charge.

### Songthaew (Shared Truck)
**THB 20-50**. White songthaew on the Na Klua-Sattahip route. Cheap but slow and may require walking from the drop-off point.

### Self-Drive
Free parking at the park. GPS to "Columbia Pictures Aquaverse" on Google Maps.

## Parking at Aquaverse

- **Cost:** Free for all guests
- **Size:** Large parking area with room for hundreds of vehicles including cars, vans, and tour buses
- **Security:** Well-lit with security personnel on site
- **Motorcycle parking:** Also available

## Travel Tips

- **Download Grab** before your trip -- it's the easiest way to get around Pattaya and to the park
- **Avoid rush hour** (7-9 AM, 5-7 PM) when traveling from Bangkok for shorter drive times
- **Book return transport** -- Arrange your return Grab/taxi before the park closes at 6 PM, as demand spikes at closing time
- **Combine with other attractions** -- Nong Nooch Tropical Garden is only 10 minutes from Aquaverse

## FAQ

### Q: Is there a shuttle bus to Aquaverse?
No, Aquaverse does not operate an official shuttle bus. Use Grab (THB 300-500 from Pattaya, 20-30 min).

### Q: Is parking free at Aquaverse?
Yes, parking is completely free for all guests.

### Q: How far is Aquaverse from Pattaya Walking Street?
About 20-30 minutes by car/Grab, approximately 25 km south along Sukhumvit Road.
