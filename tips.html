Tip #1

Before You Go

A little preparation goes a long way. Here's what to sort out before your visit.

🎫

Book Tickets Online

Always buy tickets online in advance. The gate price is around THB 1,600, but online tickets start from around THB 1,200 (check latest prices) — that's over 25% off. Online tickets also include a free locker rental (worth THB 300). Most platforms offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

📅

Check the Schedule

Aquaverse is closed every Wednesday for maintenance. Don't plan your visit on a Wednesday! The park operates 10 AM – 6 PM on all other days. Check the official Facebook page for any special closures or holiday hours before you go.

🌤️

Best Season: Nov – Apr

The dry season from November to April offers the best weather for your visit. It's sunny and warm without heavy rain. The rainy season (May–October) can bring afternoon showers, but the park is still enjoyable — and often less crowded.

Tip #2

What to Bring

Pack smart to enjoy your day without any hassles.

👙

Swimwear

Proper swimwear is required for all water attractions. Avoid clothing with buttons, zippers, or metal buckles that could damage slides or scratch you.

🧴

Sunscreen

Reef-safe, water-resistant sunscreen is a must. The Pattaya sun is intense, and you'll be outdoors all day. Reapply every 2 hours, especially after going on water rides.

🎒

Waterproof Bag

A small dry bag or waterproof pouch protects your phone, wallet, and room key. You can also rent lockers at the park (free with online tickets).

🏖️

Towel

Bring your own towel to save money. Towel rentals are available at the park but cost extra. A quick-dry microfiber towel is ideal.

👕

Change of Clothes

You'll want dry clothes for the drive home or dinner after the park. Changing rooms and showers are available near the exit.

💵

Cash (THB)

While the park uses RFID wristbands for most purchases, having some Thai Baht cash is handy for tipping, nearby restaurants, or the parking area. ATMs are available on-site.

Tip #3

What NOT to Bring

Leave these items at home or in your car — they're not allowed inside the park.

🍱

Outside Food & Drinks

No outside food or beverages are allowed inside the park. The only exceptions are baby food, baby formula, and any medically necessary dietary items. Water bottles are allowed but must be sealed.

⚽

Balls & Sports Equipment

Inflatable toys, beach balls, footballs, and other sports equipment are not permitted. The park provides tubes and floats where needed on rides and in the wave pool.

🛹

Skateboards & Scooters

Skateboards, rollerblades, scooters, and hoverboards are not allowed due to wet surfaces and safety concerns throughout the park grounds.

🪑

Folding Chairs & Tents

Personal folding chairs, pop-up tents, and large umbrellas are prohibited. The park has plenty of shaded seating areas, sun loungers, and cabanas available for rent.

Tip #4

Dress Code

What to wear (and what to avoid) for a comfortable day at the park.

Allowed

  • ✓ Swimsuits, bikinis, board shorts, rash guards
  • ✓ Water shoes or sport sandals (recommended for walking between zones)
  • ✓ Lycra/spandex activewear
  • ✓ Cover-ups for restaurant areas
  • ✓ Modest swimwear (full-coverage options welcome)

Not Allowed on Slides

  • ✗ Clothing with buttons, zippers, rivets, or metal buckles
  • ✗ Denim jeans or shorts on water slides
  • ✗ Loose jewelry, necklaces, or dangling earrings
  • ✗ GoPro mounts or selfie sticks on rides

Important Rules

  • Mandatory shower: All guests must shower before entering any water attraction
  • Life jackets: Provided free for children and non-swimmers at designated zones
  • Swim diapers: Required for all children who are not toilet trained
Tip #5

Best Time to Visit

When to go for shorter lines, better weather, and a more relaxed experience.

📆

Weekdays Are Best

Visit on a weekday (Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri) to avoid crowds. Weekends and Thai public holidays see significantly more visitors, meaning longer queues at popular slides like the Ghostbusters Water Dome and Zombieland drop.

🌅

Arrive at 10 AM

Be at the gate when it opens at 10:00 AM. The first 1–2 hours have the shortest queues. Head straight to the thrill rides before the crowds build up after lunch. The period from 1–3 PM is typically the busiest.

🚫

Avoid Wednesdays

This is the most common mistake tourists make. Aquaverse is closed every Wednesday for maintenance. Don't show up on a Wednesday — check your calendar before booking transport!

Tip #6

Money-Saving Tips

Get the most value from your Aquaverse visit without breaking the bank.

💰

Book Online (Save over 25%)

The single biggest saving is buying tickets online. Gate price is around THB 1,600, but online platforms like Klook offer tickets from around THB 1,200 (check latest prices). That's up to THB 400+ saved per person. Online tickets also include a free locker (worth THB 300).

🏖️

Skip the Cabana

Cabanas start at THB 2,500/day and while comfortable, they're not essential. There are plenty of free shaded areas and sun loungers around the park. Bring your own towel and claim a spot early instead.

📱

Use RFID Wristband

Load money onto your RFID wristband at the entrance to avoid carrying cash around the park. It works at all food stalls and shops. Any unused balance is refundable at the end of your visit — no waste!

🍽️

Eat a Late Breakfast

Have a big breakfast at your hotel before arriving. Park food is reasonably priced but adds up. A late breakfast means you can skip the lunch rush, eat a late lunch around 2 PM when restaurants are quieter, and save money.

Tip #7

Food & Dining Guide

Where to eat inside Aquaverse — note that outside food is not allowed.

🍔

Food Court & Thai Food Stalls

The park's main dining area serves burgers, grilled meats, Thai dishes, and kids' meals. The park uses a credit band system called Foodville for all purchases. Prices: THB 80–350 per item. Air-conditioned seating available.

🍗

Snack Bars

Themed snack bars throughout the park serve fried chicken, wings, rice bowls, and Thai snacks. Casual outdoor seating near the attractions. Prices: THB 80–350. Great for a quick bite between rides.

🍹

Bar Area

Poolside bar near the wave pool. Smoothies, fresh juices, cocktails (for adults), and light snacks. Perfect spot to relax in the afternoon. Drinks: THB 80–200.

Remember: Outside food and beverages are strictly prohibited. Baby food, baby formula, and medically necessary items are the only exceptions. Sealed water bottles are allowed.

Tip #8

Best Order to Visit Zones

Our recommended route to maximize your time and minimize queueing.

Morning (10:00 AM – 12:30 PM) — Hit the Thrills

  1. Zombieland — Head here first for the 18-meter extreme drop slide. Queues build fast after 11 AM.
  2. Ghostbusters — The Water Dome and thrill slides are best experienced before crowds arrive.
  3. Jumanji — Jungle-themed rapids and adventure slides. Moderate wait times in the morning.

Midday (12:30 PM – 2:30 PM) — Eat & Explore

  1. Lunch break — Eat at the food court or themed snack bars. Beat the 12 PM rush by eating at 12:30–1:00 PM.
  2. Bad Boys — Go-karts and dry activities. Good to do while digesting lunch.
  3. The Emoji Movie — Mini golf and photo ops. Low-intensity fun for early afternoon.

Afternoon (2:30 PM – 6:00 PM) — Relax & Enjoy

  1. Surf's Up — Try the FlowRider surf simulator. Afternoon queues are typically shorter.
  2. Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs — Lazy river. Perfect for winding down in the afternoon heat.
  3. Mega Wave Pool — End your day here with waves and the Omnitheatre entertainment.
Tip #9

For Families with Kids

Essential tips for visiting Aquaverse with little ones.

🏰

Start with Hotel Transylvania

This zone is designed for families and young children. It features SE Asia's largest kids' water playground with 9 kid-friendly slides, splash pads, dozens of water features, and character meet-and-greets with Drac, Mavis, and Blobby. Head here first while kids are full of energy.

📏

Check Height Restrictions

Some thrill rides have minimum height requirements (Zombieland zone requires 120 cm minimum). Check the height boards at each ride entrance before queueing to avoid disappointment. Hotel Transylvania and the wave pool have no height restrictions.

🎉

Foam Party Schedule

The park hosts regular foam parties in the Hotel Transylvania zone (usually at 1 PM and 3 PM on weekends). Kids love them! Check the daily schedule board at the entrance for exact times and character appearances.

👶

Baby Essentials

Baby changing facilities are available in all restrooms. Swim diapers are mandatory for non-toilet-trained children. You can bring baby food and formula — the only exception to the no outside food policy. Free life jackets are provided at water attractions.

Tip #10

Rainy Day Tips

Don't let rain ruin your plans — a water park in the rain can actually be a great experience.

🌧️

Light Rain = Still Fun

You're already getting wet at a water park! Light to moderate rain means shorter queues as many visitors leave. Slides and pools operate normally during light rain. Only lightning and severe thunderstorms cause temporary ride closures.

📱

Check the Forecast

Pattaya's rainy season (May–October) typically brings afternoon showers lasting 1–2 hours, not all-day rain. Check the weather forecast on the morning of your visit. If rain is predicted, it usually clears up by mid-afternoon.

🏛️

Covered Areas Exist

The park has several covered areas including the food court area, indoor sections of the Hotel Transylvania zone, the Omnitheatre, and covered walkways. The go-kart track and mini golf continue in light rain too.

Tip #11

Photo Tips

How to capture the best memories without worrying about your gear.

📸

RFID Auto-Capture

Your RFID wristband links to the park's automatic photo capture system. Cameras at major ride exits and splash zones take your photo automatically. You can preview and purchase digital packages at kiosks throughout the park or at the exit. No need to bring your camera on rides!

📱

Waterproof Phone Case

A waterproof phone case (IPX8 rated, available at 7-Eleven for THB 100–200) lets you take photos and videos on rides without risking water damage. Test it in a sink before your trip. Alternatively, leave your phone in the locker and rely on the park's RFID photo system.

Pro tip: The best photo spots are the Ghostbusters Water Dome entrance, the Jumanji bridge, character meet-and-greets in Hotel Transylvania, and the wave pool sunset view. GoPros and selfie sticks are not allowed on rides but fine in walking areas.

Tip #12

Cabana — Worth It?

A breakdown of whether renting a private cabana is worth the cost.

Pros

  • ✓ Private shaded area with comfortable seating
  • ✓ Towels and toiletries included
  • ✓ Secure storage for valuables (separate from lockers)
  • ✓ Dedicated attendant service
  • ✓ Priority food and drink ordering
  • ✓ Great for groups of 4–6 to split costs

Cons

  • ✗ Expensive: starts at THB 2,500/day
  • ✗ Premium locations (wave pool side) cost THB 3,500+
  • ✗ Plenty of free shaded areas available
  • ✗ You'll spend most of your time on rides, not at the cabana
  • ✗ Locker (free with online ticket) stores valuables just fine

Our verdict: Skip the cabana unless you're visiting with elderly family members, very young children, or in a group of 4+ willing to split the cost. For most visitors, the free locker and shaded seating areas are perfectly adequate.

Tip #13

Safety Tips

Stay safe while having fun at Aquaverse.

👀

Supervise Children

Always keep children within arm's reach at water attractions, even in shallow areas. Lifeguards are stationed throughout the park, but parental supervision is essential. Free life jackets are available for young children and non-swimmers.

📋

Follow Ride Rules

Listen to ride attendant instructions and follow all posted safety signs. Height and weight restrictions exist for your safety. Don't attempt to go down slides head-first or stand up on rides unless instructed.

💧

Stay Hydrated

The Thai heat is intense. Drink water regularly, even if you don't feel thirsty — you won't notice dehydration while surrounded by water. Water refill stations are located throughout the park. Take breaks in shaded areas.

🏥

First Aid

A fully staffed first aid station is located near the main entrance. Staff can help with minor injuries, sunburn, insect bites, and other medical needs. For emergencies, the park has direct contact with local hospitals.

Tip #14

After the Park

What to do after your Aquaverse adventure — great options nearby.

🍽️

Nearby Restaurants

Several seafood restaurants are within a 5–10 minute drive along the Na Jomtien coast road. Rim Talay Seafood and Glass House are popular choices for beachside dinner with sunset views. Expect THB 200–500 per person.

🌺

Nong Nooch Garden

Nong Nooch Tropical Garden is just 10 minutes away from Aquaverse. This world-famous botanical garden features stunning landscaping, cultural shows, and elephant experiences. Open until 6 PM, making it a potential same-day combo.

🌅

Sunset at Jomtien

Jomtien Beach is 20 minutes north of Aquaverse. Arrive by 5:30 PM for spectacular sunset views. Walk along the promenade, grab a coconut from a beach vendor, and enjoy the evening breeze after your water park day.

Tip #15

Bonus Quick Tips

A few more insider tips to make your visit perfect.

  • WiFi: Free WiFi is available throughout the park, but it can be slow during peak hours. Download maps and info beforehand.
  • Lockers: Free with online tickets. Located near the entrance. Large enough for a backpack. You can access them unlimited times.
  • Parking: Free for all guests. The car park is large and rarely full. Motorcycle parking is also available.
  • Closing time: Rides close at 5:45 PM, but the park stays open until 6:00 PM for shopping, photos, and exiting.
  • Re-entry: Re-entry is generally not permitted once you exit. Make sure to bring everything you need inside.
  • Lost items: Check the lost and found desk near the main entrance. The park also has a phone number for inquiries after your visit.
