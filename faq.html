Tickets & Pricing

Everything about ticket costs, discounts, and booking options.

Walk-in gate price is THB 1,595 (~$44 USD) per person. Online advance tickets start from THB 870 (~$24 USD), saving you up to 45%. Children under 3 years old enter free. Online tickets include locker rental (worth THB 300).

Children under 3 years old enter free. Children aged 3 and above require a full-price ticket. Online tickets for children (3+) start from THB 870. There is no separate child pricing — the same ticket price applies to all guests aged 3 and above.

Online tickets are significantly cheaper. Gate price is THB 1,595 while online prices start from THB 870 — that's up to 45% off. Online tickets also include a free locker rental (worth THB 300) and instant mobile voucher confirmation. We strongly recommend booking online through platforms like Klook or KKday.

Locker rental is included free with online advance tickets. If you buy tickets at the gate, locker rental costs an additional THB 300. Lockers are located near the entrance and can be accessed unlimited times throughout the day using your RFID wristband.

Yes, group pricing is available for parties of 10 or more. Group bookings should be arranged directly through the park's sales team. School groups, corporate outings, and tour operators may receive additional discounts. Contact the park at +66 (0) 33 004 999 for group rate inquiries.

Cabana rental starts at THB 2,500 per day for a standard cabana (fits 4–6 people). Premium cabanas near the wave pool cost THB 3,500+. Cabanas include shade, comfortable seating, towels, a personal attendant, and priority food service. Reservations are recommended, especially on weekends.

Category 2

Opening Hours & Schedule

When the park is open, when it's closed, and when to visit.

Aquaverse is open daily from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Rides close at 5:45 PM to allow for safe park closure. Last admission is at 3:30 PM. The park is closed every Wednesday for scheduled maintenance.

Yes. Columbia Pictures Aquaverse is closed every Wednesday for scheduled maintenance. This is the most common mistake tourists make — always double-check the day before heading to the park! The park operates normally on all other days (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday through Sunday).

Last admission is at 3:30 PM. After this time, no new guests are admitted. All rides close at 5:45 PM, and the park gates close at 6:00 PM. We recommend arriving at 10 AM opening to maximize your time — a half-day visit starting at 3:30 PM is very short.

Yes, Aquaverse is generally open on Thai public holidays (except if the holiday falls on a Wednesday). Holiday periods like Songkran, New Year, and school holidays tend to be busier with potentially extended hours. Check the park's official Facebook page or call +66 (0) 33 004 999 for specific holiday schedules.

For the best experience:

  • Best day: Weekdays (Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri) are least crowded
  • Best season: November to April (dry season) has the best weather
  • Best arrival time: 10 AM at gate opening for shortest queues
  • Avoid: Weekends, Thai school holidays, and Songkran week

Getting There & Parking

How to reach Aquaverse from Bangkok, Pattaya, and the airports.

Multiple options available:

  • By car: 1.5–2 hours via Motorway Route 7 toward Pattaya (free parking at park)
  • By bus: From Ekkamai Bus Terminal (next to Ekkamai BTS), then taxi from Pattaya
  • By shared van: From Victory Monument or Ekkamai, ~THB 240/person
  • By Grab/taxi: THB 1,200–1,500 + tolls from Bangkok

See complete transportation guide →

Aquaverse is about 30 minutes south of Pattaya city center in Na Jomtien:

  • By Grab/taxi: THB 200–400 from central Pattaya
  • By songthaew: Take the Na Jomtien route (cheapest option)
  • Hotel shuttle: Some hotels offer shuttle services — check with your hotel

The park address is 888 Moo 8, Sukhumvit Road, Na Chom Thian, Sattahip, Chon Buri 20250.

There is no official park shuttle bus. However, many Pattaya hotels and tour operators offer shuttle services or day trip packages that include round-trip transportation. Grab and Bolt ride-hailing apps are the most convenient and affordable way to reach the park from Pattaya or nearby areas.

Yes, parking is completely free for all guests. The car park is large with space for cars, vans, and tour buses. Motorcycle parking is also available. The parking area is located directly in front of the main entrance.

  • From Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK): 1.5–2 hours by car/taxi via Motorway Route 7, approximately THB 1,500–2,000
  • From U-Tapao Airport (UTP): Only 20–30 minutes by car/taxi, approximately THB 300–500. This is the closest airport to Aquaverse
  • From Don Mueang Airport (DMK): 2–2.5 hours by car/taxi, approximately THB 1,800–2,500

What to Bring & Wear

Dress code, packing essentials, and prohibited items.

Proper swimwear is required for all water attractions:

  • Allowed: Swimsuits, bikinis, board shorts, rash guards, lycra activewear
  • Not allowed on slides: Clothing with buttons, zippers, or metal buckles; denim; loose jewelry
  • Recommended: Water shoes for walking between zones; cover-ups for restaurant areas

All guests must shower before entering water attractions.

Read full dress code guide →

Yes, you can and should bring your own towel to save money. Towel rental is available at the park for an additional fee. A quick-dry microfiber towel is ideal as it packs small, dries fast, and takes up minimal locker space.

No, outside food and beverages are not allowed inside the park. The only exceptions are:

  • Baby food and baby formula
  • Medically necessary dietary items
  • Sealed water bottles

The park has multiple restaurants and snack bars serving Thai and international food at reasonable prices (THB 100–350 per dish).

Standard lockers are large enough to fit a backpack, change of clothes, shoes, and personal items. They are located near the main entrance and can be accessed unlimited times throughout the day using your RFID wristband. Locker rental is free with online tickets or THB 300 at the gate.

The following items are not permitted inside the park:

  • Outside food and drinks (except baby food, sealed water)
  • Glass containers
  • Skateboards, scooters, rollerblades, hoverboards
  • Folding chairs and pop-up tents
  • Inflatable toys and beach balls
  • Sharp objects and weapons
  • Alcohol brought from outside
  • Drones
  • Professional photography/video equipment

GoPros and selfie sticks are not allowed on rides but are fine in walking areas.

Category 5

Rides & Attractions

Height restrictions, ride details, and what to expect.

Height restrictions vary by ride:

  • Most thrill slides: 110–140 cm minimum
  • Zombieland extreme slides: 140 cm minimum
  • FlowRider surf simulator: 110 cm minimum
  • Hotel Transylvania kids zone: No height restriction
  • Wave pool & lazy river: No height restriction

Height measurement boards are posted at each ride entrance. Free life jackets are provided for children who don't meet requirements at certain attractions.

View all rides with height requirements →

Most slides can be ridden solo. Some raft rides require 2–4 riders — in these cases, single riders are grouped with other guests. The FlowRider surf simulator is a solo experience. Check with ride attendants at each attraction for specific requirements.

The Bad Boys go-kart track requires riders to be at least 12 years old to drive solo and meet a minimum height of 140 cm. Children aged 6–11 can ride as passengers with an adult driver in a two-seater kart. Go-karts are a separate paid activity (not included in standard park admission, approximately THB 200–300 per session).

The FlowRider surf simulator has two modes:

  • Bodyboarding (beginner-friendly): Most guests can do this on their first try. Just lie on the board and enjoy the ride.
  • Stand-up surfing (advanced): Requires balance and practice. Expect to fall a few times! Instructors are available to help.

No prior surfing experience is needed for bodyboarding. Minimum height is 110 cm. Queue times are typically 10–20 minutes.

The best zones for young children are:

  • Hotel Transylvania: SE Asia's largest kids' water playground with 9 kid-friendly slides, splash pads, 100+ water features, and character meet-and-greets
  • Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs: Gentle lazy river suitable for all ages
  • Mega Wave Pool: Shallow areas perfect for toddlers and young swimmers
  • The Emoji Movie: Mini golf and photo ops (dry activities)

Yes! Aquaverse offers several dry attractions:

  • Bad Boys Go-Karts: Racing track (separate fee)
  • The Emoji Movie Mini Golf: 18-hole themed course
  • Onsen spa area: Japanese-style hot spring relaxation
  • Live shows & entertainment: At the Omnitheatre by the wave pool
  • Character meet-and-greets: Photo ops with movie characters

Category 6

Food & Dining

Restaurant options, dietary requirements, and the payment system.

Aquaverse has several dining options:

  • Firehouse Grill (Ghostbusters zone): Burgers, grilled meats, Thai dishes, kids' meals. THB 120–350. Air-conditioned.
  • Chicken Joe's (Surf's Up zone): Fried chicken, wings, rice bowls, Thai snacks. THB 100–250.
  • Tiki Bar (Wave Pool area): Smoothies, juices, cocktails, light snacks. THB 80–200.
  • Snack carts & ice cream stands located throughout the park.

Read our food guide with recommendations →

While there is no dedicated halal-certified restaurant at Aquaverse, several food options are suitable for Muslim visitors including seafood, grilled chicken, and vegetable dishes. Staff can advise on ingredients. Pork items are clearly marked on menus. For strict halal requirements, consider eating at a halal-certified restaurant before arriving at the park.

Yes, vegetarian options are available at most food outlets, including veggie burgers, salads, rice dishes, fresh fruit, and Thai vegetable stir-fries. Vegan options are more limited but available upon request — ask staff for dairy-free and egg-free choices. The Tiki Bar offers fresh fruit smoothies and juices that are naturally vegan.

Upon entry, guests receive an RFID wristband that can be loaded with credit at top-up stations throughout the park:

  • Load credit using cash or credit card at kiosks near the entrance and food areas
  • Tap your wristband at all food stalls, shops, and optional activities to pay
  • Any unused balance is refunded at exit kiosks when you leave
  • The wristband also links to the park's automatic ride photo system

No need to carry a wallet around the park!

Category 7

Facilities & Services

WiFi, first aid, accessibility, and other park services.

Yes, free WiFi is available throughout the park. However, speeds can be slow during peak hours with many users connected. For reliable connectivity, consider using your mobile data. We recommend downloading any maps or information you need before entering the park.

Yes, a fully staffed first aid station is located near the main entrance. Trained staff can assist with minor injuries, sunburn, insect bites, heat-related issues, and other medical needs. For emergencies, the park has direct communication with local hospitals. Lifeguards are stationed at all water attractions throughout the park.

Report lost items to the Lost and Found desk located near the main entrance. Staff regularly collect items found throughout the park. For inquiries after your visit, call +66 (0) 33 004 999. To prevent loss, we strongly recommend using the lockers (free with online tickets) to store valuables rather than carrying them around the park.

Aquaverse provides accessible features including:

  • Accessible pathways and ramps throughout most park areas
  • Accessible restrooms available
  • Wave pool viewing areas, restaurants, shows, and walking-area attractions are all accessible
  • Some water rides may have physical requirements that limit accessibility

Contact the park in advance at +66 (0) 33 004 999 for specific accessibility questions and to arrange any needed assistance.

Re-entry is generally not permitted once you exit the park. Make sure to bring everything you need inside. If you need to retrieve something from your car, speak with guest services at the exit — they may be able to arrange temporary re-entry on a case-by-case basis.
